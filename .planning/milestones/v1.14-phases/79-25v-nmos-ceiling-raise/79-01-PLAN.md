<objective>
Re-run the NMOS-01 hardware gate with the CORRECTED electrical methodology (per CONTEXT.md D-03/D-04): measure the shield's achievable VPP on the **direct VPE path** (the 0x0B / EPROM_LEGACY path the 4 NMOS chips actually use) at **maximum potentiometer**, chip-OUT, with the operator DMM as the authoritative reading. This supersedes the prior 79-01 NOT-CLEARED verdict, which measured the wrong rail (`firestarter vpp` always forces the DROPPED 0x07/0x08 path — firestarter/src/hardware_operations.cpp:28 — and read ~12V, which is not what these chips see).

Purpose: The firmware does NOT enforce a VPP ceiling — `eprom_check_vpp` (firestarter/src/proms/eprom.cpp:209-272) only blocks OVER-voltage and WARNS-but-proceeds on UNDER-voltage. So a physical chip-OUT measurement on the correct rail is the only real safety boundary between a 25V-declared chip and an under-driven write. Per D-05, >= 25V on the direct VPE path at max pot is a HARD pre-gate: a CLEARED verdict is the sole authorization for the 79-02 ceiling raise and the 79-03 graduation.

Output: 79-01-SUMMARY.md recording the gate evidence and verdict.
</objective>
