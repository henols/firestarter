Open the bench. The operator attaches the Leonardo, seats the Winbond W27C512 and reads the shield silkscreen;
Claude then establishes what image is actually on the board by flashing the tree under test and identifying it
by commit and verified byte count, and confirms the seated part by chip-id before a single byte is spent.

image cannot even connect, and the v1.31 version string identifies nothing. D-01 exists because the ST
M27C512 and the Winbond W27C512 are both called "512", both 28-pin `0x07`, and differ in erasability and in
VPP. Both questions are settled here, before Gate 2's authorized spend.

Output: Gate 1's identity table filled with measured values, the reflash proof recorded, and the chip-id gate
cleared.
