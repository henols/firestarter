`img3.bin`, each with the full three-verdict oracle set and its own read-stability check, then the gate is
verdicted against D-09's pass rule with the re-seat ledger and the no-`--force` source assertion recorded.

clean pass is simultaneously the byte-exactness proof and positive proof the erase fired. D-09's rule is 3/3
on both oracles, and D-14 leaves no room to soften anything short of that.

Output: cycle-2 and cycle-3 subsections with separate oracle verdicts, `runs/cycle2/` and `runs/cycle3/`
evidence, the re-seat ledger, and a closed `Gate 2 verdict:` line.
