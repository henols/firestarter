<locked_values>
These are LOCKED. Do not re-derive them, and do not copy the row values from
`140-RESEARCH.md` § Code Examples verbatim — **that example ships `0x07 overprogram_factor = 3`,
which is superseded**.

| protocol | overprogram_cap_us | energy_cap_us | max_pulses | overprogram_factor | verify_mode | vpp_path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0x07` | `75000` | `0` | `25` | **`0`** | `VERIFY_PER_PULSE_PLUS_FINAL` | `VPP_PATH_DROP_RESISTOR` |
| `0x08` | `75000` | `0` | `25` | `0` | `VERIFY_PER_PULSE_PLUS_FINAL` | `VPP_PATH_DROP_RESISTOR` |
| `0x0B` | `75000` | `50000` | `255` | `0` | `VERIFY_PER_PULSE` | `VPP_PATH_DIRECT_VPE` |

Why `0x07 overprogram_factor = 0` (operator-decided 2026-08-09, this session):
1. Behaviour-preserving. No protocol overprograms today — `src/proms/eprom.cpp:161-178` is retry
   escalation of `pulse_delay`, not an Intel-style 3N margin pulse. Shipping `3` would be an
   unvalidated behaviour change to all 170 chips in the row.
2. All three `0x07` datasheets read (Winbond W27C512 Rev A4, ST M27C512 Rev 3, Microchip 27C512A
   DS11173G) specify no overprogram — 113 of the row's 170 chips.
3. It applies D-06's precedent consistently: a primary datasheet beats `PROJECT.md`'s derived
   throughput table.

**The named, scoped divergence that MUST stay visible (F-140-05):** the 22 Intel-family 1 ms parts on
`0x07` (Intel 2764 / 2764A / 27128 / 27128A / 27512, TI TMS2764, NEC UPD2764, ST M2764A, and the
rest of the 1000 µs sub-population) genuinely do want a `3 × N` margin pulse. Serving them correctly
requires splitting `0x07`, which this phase's "no second dispatch key" constraint (TABLE-05) forbids.
This is a **recorded divergence from `PROJECT.md`'s throughput table plus a follow-up candidate for
Phase 146** — never a silent omission.

Also locked: `overprogram_cap_us` stays in the table even though it is inert on every row, because
TABLE-01 names the column. `75000` uniformly on all three rows keeps the figure publicly stated on
gh#15 ("the 32-bit-safe helper is needed for the 75 ms overprogram pulse") true.
