**D-13 — VPP-04's premise does not hold, and this plan is where that is discharged and recorded.**
VPP-04 as written says the refusal is "re-verified against the **existing** gate rather than assumed
intact." Verified by grep: `MSG_ERR_VPP_HIGH` and `MSG_WARN_VPP_HIGH` appear in **no** test anywhere
under `test/` or `tests/`. `test_val_eprom` pins `handle->vpp_mv = 0` against a `0`-returning stub
(`test_val_eprom.cpp:74`) precisely so the comparison never fires; `test_flash_intel_vpp` is a
different protocol family (`0x10`) and per correction C-2 it runs in **no** PlatformIO environment and
SIGABRTs after case 1, so its own assertions have never been observed to execute; `native_loop_v131`
does not mock `rurp_read_voltage_mv` at all. The requirement is discharged by **authoring** the gate
and recording that correction — not by pointing at a gate for another family.

**D-15 — the gate must prove three things, not one:** (a) an injected out-of-range reading produces
`MSG_ERR_VPP_HIGH` (`0xB8`) plus `RESPONSE_CODE_ERROR`; (b) **no HV route is left asserted** on that
refusal path; (c) the `FLAG_FORCE` leg still downgrades to `MSG_WARN_VPP_HIGH` (`0x82`) plus
`RESPONSE_CODE_WARNING`, since that **is** the refusal's semantics. A refusal-only gate was rejected:
it would pass even if the rewrite left the rail energised on the refusal path, which is the exact
failure VPP-02 exists to prevent.

**All of (a), (b) and (c) are GREEN ON ARRIVAL and prove nothing until seen RED.** `eprom_check_vpp`
has exactly one `return` (`:337`, the Rev-0 warning) and it fires **before** any route is asserted;
every other path — nominal, over-voltage ERROR (`:370-371`), over-voltage-with-`FLAG_FORCE`
(`:367-368`), under-voltage (`:389-390`) — falls through to the unconditional clear at `:393`
(correction C-3). This is therefore a **regression** gate, and a plan that presented (b) as newly
established behaviour would be overclaiming. Each of the three legs gets its own named planted
violation and its transcript verbatim in the SUMMARY (the standing D-15 discipline: 12 planted runs in
Phase 140, 13 in Phase 141).

**D-14** places the gate in a new suite inside the **existing** `[env:native_loop_v131]`, already
wired by plan 142-01. **The honest cost, to be restated in the phase record:** `native_loop_v131` runs
in **no CI leg of either repository**. This gate is a local run-by-name obligation, never implied CI
coverage — the same terms `native_params_v131`, `native_trace_v131` and `native_loop_v131` already
carry.

**D-03 non-claim, applies here:** nothing in this plan says anything about silicon. The refusal is
proven against an **injected** reading through a mock; no ADC is read in any native suite, so "the
drop resistor produces ~13 V" and "the refusal blocks on real silicon at a real over-voltage" are both

**Also authored here, and for the same reason — pre-rewrite baselines for VPP-03 (research assumption
A3).** Plan 142-04 converts the four hand-rolled disables at `eprom.cpp:174`, `:327`, `:393` and `:409`
into the shared `EPROM_HV_ALL_OFF_MASK` composite. Research argues those conversions are byte-identical

assert byte-identity in prose, measure it.** If the argument is wrong, `CMD_ERASE` and
`CMD_CHECK_CHIP_ID` behaviour moves, which `PROJECT.md:189-190` forbids. Task 2 therefore pins both
commands' current control-value streams as measured literals **before** the rewrite, so plan 142-04's
green run is the measurement.
