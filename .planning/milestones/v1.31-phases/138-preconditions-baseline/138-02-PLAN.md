Discharge PREP-04's deliverables: re-derive the live per-protocol pulse-width distribution for `0x07`,
`0x08` and `0x0B` from the shipped `chip_database.json` as a **committed, reproducible, self-checking

GitHub comment on gh#15**. A number a stranger cannot re-run is the weakest link in that comment. The
figures must be measured in this milestone, not restated from the seed — so the script re-derives and the
artifact **compares**, treating any divergence from the seed or from `138-RESEARCH.md` as a finding
rather than a reconciliation.

**Independence:** `chip_database.json` and `database.py` are byte-identical across every candidate tree,
so PREP-04 does **not** depend on PREP-01 or PREP-02. This plan runs in wave 1 and touches no branch.
