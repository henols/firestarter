<objective>
Freeze the captured pre-change trace: paste the three empirically-dumped merged streams into
`eprom_v131_expected.h` as literal arrays, switch the three protocol cases from soundness assertions to
full ordered positional equality, and pin the whole thing with the proven three-part immutability triple
— fixture, committed inventory, and a python identity gate (**D-04**).

Purpose: **D-01** requires a real trace fixture rather than a blob-SHA inventory, because Phase 144's
TEST-06 criterion — every changed strobe attributable to a named decision — needs something to diff. A
blob SHA proves file identity and would leave TEST-06 with nothing. But a blob SHA alone also cannot
distinguish "unchanged" from "an array deleted together with the assertions that consumed it", which is
why the inventory and the consumer-inclusion check exist alongside it.

Output: three frozen arrays, an inventory JSON, a parallel identity gate, and the gate observed failing
before it is trusted.

**Fence:** no write-path source is edited. `src/proms/eprom.cpp` and `src/proms/memory.cpp` stay
untouched, and `firestarter/tests/test_golden_trace_identity.py` — the existing SDP gate — is **not**
refactored, parameterised, or edited.
</objective>
