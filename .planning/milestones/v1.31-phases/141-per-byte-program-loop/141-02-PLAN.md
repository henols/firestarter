The hazard is live today, not gated on Phase 143: `pulse-delay` is parsed by `extract_long` into an
unclamped `uint32_t` (`src/json_parser.c:503`), unlike the read-timing knobs which clamp to 1000 us.

overflowing at 16384 — producing a **silently short** pulse (a 20000 us request becomes roughly
3615 us), which on real silicon under-programs the byte and then reports a *verify* failure.

Purpose: make the over-ceiling pulse impossible on the primitive every protocol reaches, and make the
split arithmetic unit-testable without elapsed time.
