<objective>
Run positions 7 and 8 — **W29C040**, whose recorded failure sits inside the region this command
deliberately never writes, and **W29C020**, the healthiest 32-pin part and the supplier of the
256 KiB class ceiling.

Purpose: these two share an algorithm and a region policy, and both of their write ranges are
**carved out** — the first and last sixteen kibibytes are excluded by the flash algorithm's own
boot-block handling, so neither part is written full-device. For W29C040 that carve-out is the whole
divergence story: the address its permanent failure is recorded at lies **inside** the excluded
range, so this command cannot reproduce, refute or even reach the known fault. That is precisely
what `no comparable baseline` means, and booking it before the run means the control arbitration is
budgeted rather than discovered. W29C020, by contrast, was run on this very rig on the v1.33 arm
three plans ago, which gives it the strongest baseline in the sweep and makes it the right part to
set the 256 KiB ceiling from.

Output: two provenance records, three copied-out report pairs including W29C040's control
arbitration, two primary rows, one control row, the phase's first measured 256 KiB `dev test`
figure, and the eight-of-ten mark.
</objective>
