**PD-2 — RESEARCH R6 option (A): `render_steps.py` learns a second section, and the live-gate
count stays at 7.** The `C-NN` list goes under a **new H2** (`## Chip-sweep step list`); a `C-NN`
heading inside `## Step list` raises `ProcedureParseError` on `_STEP_ID_RE = ^P-(\d\d)$` and reds
the existing gate. Option (B) — a new H2 with no renderer change — is rejected: D-07 asks for the
empty-diff gate to be re-confirmed, and re-confirming a gate that does not cover the new text is
the vacuous-pass shape this project keeps naming. **The C-section diff is added inside the existing
`render_steps.py` live-gate block, not as an eighth gate**, so the suite target is exactly
**14/14 selftests + 7/7 live gates** (12+2 tools, 5+2 gates).

**PD-3 — RESEARCH R3 option (A): copy out, then remove.** `dev test` writes its report into the
frozen `FIRESTARTER_CONFIG_DIR`, and `check_arms.compute_config_dir_sha` walks
`root.rglob("*")` filtered to `is_file()` — so one report changes the pinned
`77adfdd26ed8710c4a70882e6dc9ee7bb494286fe225000d581f9e730dd77ad0`. An **empty** `reports/`
directory is SHA-invisible, so copying both files out and removing them restores the digest
exactly (proven empirically in R3). `append_chip_evidence.py` asserts the pristine digest **before**
it derives and **again** after it removes. Options (B) a second config dir and (C) excluding
`reports/` from the walk are both rejected in R3 with their costs named.

**PD-4 — RESEARCH R7 option (A): extend the map and derive `_CHIP_CHOICES` from it.** This plan's

**PD-5 — AM27C020 is booked `diverges: no comparable baseline` before the bench, not after.**
CONTEXT.md D-03 left this to the planner; RESEARCH R1 settles it on four independent grounds, any
one sufficient: v1.18's figures are a **64 B** ramp write at `0x1da00`/`0x16600` obtained with
`write -b` (now on the forbidden table — the same ground D-03 already accepts for FM1608), against
`dev test`'s **256 B** `P = C & D` masked slot at `0x3FF00`; and v1.18's disposition is `DEFER`,
which is not a per-step verdict D-02 can flip against. So **N ≥ 4** before any genuine flip
(W29C040, FM1608, ST M27C512, AM27C020) — budget for four control re-runs, do not discover them.
The row must also state that AM27C020's control re-run is **guaranteed non-arbitrating**: under
D-17 the control run lands on the **next slot down** (`0x3FE00`), and the part is on record as
non-deterministic, so a v1.33-vs-control difference cannot distinguish arm from silicon.

**PD-6 — the shared `-b`-semantics clause, hoisted once.** Five of the ten parts' newest

once as a shared clause of every `no comparable baseline` reason; do not re-argue it per row.

**PD-7 — read-divergence follow-up runs automatically on every class, and is never a prompt.**

the divergence metric survives. When a position's report carries
`read_divergence.repeat_divergent == true`, run **one** follow-up
`dev consistency-check <CHIP_TOKEN> --runs 3 --keep-files --output-dir <position reads dir>` on the

`read_consistency_followup` column as a data point against the standing A2 N=3 question. Cost:
~33 s at 64 KiB, ~2.3 min at 256 KiB, ~4.4 min at 512 KiB, and only on a divergent position.
**Do not ask the operator whether to run it** — RESEARCH offered that option and it is exactly the
artificial "continue?" gate 161 D-02 forbids. Do **not** attempt to close the A2 question.

**PD-15 — the kill log is the load-bearing half.** A ceiling kill runs under a **numbered** log
with full stdout **and** stderr captured, the last progress frame captured, and the record reading
`timed out at N s against a measured baseline of M s`. Two `dev test`-specific additions: write
progress is **time-keyed per block with the clock restarting each block**, so the last frame names
a **block, not a byte offset**; and one `dev test` invocation covers twelve steps behind one
timeout, so the log must also name **which step was in flight** — line-stamp the invocation or, at
minimum, record the wall-clock at which the last console line appeared. Phase 160's single
*unlogged* 120 s kill is what produced the untraceable `~/.firestarter` contamination this
milestone still carries.

**PD-16 — the divergence rule, operationalised.** Per position, in this order:
1. Read `step_verdicts` from the copied-out report.
2. Skip every step `derive_plan` marks structurally **NA** for that part (`DERIVE-PLAN.json` is the
   authority). An NA is never a flip.
3. Skip every step the **prior sweep never measured**. That is not a flip either; it is recorded
   inside `prior_dispositions_all` as "no comparable prior measurement".
4. For the remaining steps apply **D-02**: any comparable per-step flip in either direction is
   `diverges: <step> <old>→<new>`.
5. Apply **D-05** only to the three parts the record calls **deterministic** — W27E512 (bit 7
   @0x3d), W27E040 (bit 4 @0x7db), W29C040 (timeout @0x0000ff): a moved offset or byte is
   `diverges: symptom moved <from> → <to>`. AM27C020 is recorded non-deterministic, so its symptom
   variance goes in `anomalies` and never triggers.
6. Apply **D-03** where no comparable baseline exists: `diverges: no comparable baseline — <why>`,
   with PD-6's shared `-b` clause cited rather than re-argued.
7. **D-01**: the headline is keyed on the **write path**; every prior sweep that touched the part is
   cited in `prior_dispositions_all` regardless.

**PD-17 — one meter reading per group; every other row records a named absence, never a copied
number.** D-13 puts the meter out exactly twice (the 12 V group, then the 13 V pair). The position
at which the meter is actually read carries the operator's figure in `vpp_real_mv`. **Every other
position in that group records `vpp_real_mv` as** `not measured — meter read once for the <12 V|13 V>
group at position <position_id> (D-13); this position's real rail is derivable from
vpp_firmware_mv and the group's measured meter-to-firmware ratio <R>`. That shape is a valid
non-null under `gate_record._is_acceptable_not_measured`, and it keeps a group figure from being
presented as a per-position measurement. Every position still takes its **own** firmware VPP
reading — exactly one, no monitor loop.

**PD-20 — for W27C512 and W29C020 the most recent recorded disposition is v1.34's own cell A3/B2,
not v1.16.** D-04 says a row compares against the **most recent** recorded disposition with v1.15 as
the floor, and names which milestone it came from. Phase 161's cell A3/B2 recorded both of these
parts on **this exact rig, on the v1.33 arm, with a full-device write, read and judged verify, and
with no forbidden flag** — rows `A3-B2__v133__w27c512` and `A3-B2__v133__w29c020` in
`.planning/v1.34/bench/EVIDENCE.jsonl`, both `outcome: validated`. That is strictly the strongest
comparison available for either part, and for W29C020 it also removes PD-6's problem entirely,
because the v1.16 disposition for that part was obtained with a now-forbidden flag while A3/B2's was
not. So: **`prior_disposition_source` for these two parts names v1.34 cell A3/B2**, and the v1.16
ledger entry is cited alongside in `prior_dispositions_all` as the newest **pre-v1.34** disposition.

The comparable steps from an A3/B2 row are `read` (its three-run consistency set, one distinct
digest on the v1.33 arm), and `write` **and** `verify` jointly, because a judged full-device digest
match means the write landed and read back correctly. `id`, `erase` and `blank-check` have **no
comparable measurement** in a write-read-verify row and are recorded as such — never flips. Note the
vocabulary difference on the row itself: A3/B2's verdict is a judged digest match, not a per-step
verdict set, so the mapping onto D-02's step names is stated in `prior_dispositions_all` rather than
assumed.

**PD-18 — plan/wave granularity.** A wave is a pot/JP4 group by default (CONTEXT.md's discretion
list), and each bench plan is its own wave because two bench plans can never run in parallel on one
rig. The two four-part groups are each split across **two** plans purely on **context cost** — the
only legitimate split reason available to a planner here. **Both split points cost zero physical
actions**: 162-05→162-06 splits between parts 2 and 3 (no pot move, no JP4 change), and
162-07→162-08 splits between parts 6 and 7 (likewise). No handover is added by the split.
