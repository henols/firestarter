permits both bit directions — so it takes two full-device 8 KiB writes with **different** patterns,

single-byte verify mismatch rather than a write error.

## Prior dispositions — part 4, FM1608

- **Newest recorded disposition (D-04):** **PASS** — *"read N=3 + write-cycle A→B (`write -b` FRAM
  method) both byte-identical to v1.15 baseline; neg-control verify(A) RC=1"*.
  Source: **v1.16 Phase 90**, `.planning/v1.16/ledger/PROTOCOL-LEDGER.md:31`. **Note the source
  milestone: v1.16, not the v1.15 row CONTEXT.md's D-03 cites** — the disposition is newer than
  CONTEXT.md assumed, and the row must name v1.16.
- **All prior sweeps that touched the part (D-01):** v1.15 Phase 81 read sweep,
  `.planning/v1.15/bench/EVIDENCE.md:59` (**PASS**, N=3, with a recorded blank-check tooling gap on
  this family flagged for the Phase 84 review — a **tooling** gap, not a read fault); v1.15 Phase 82
  A→B write, `:101` (**PASS**, overwrite proof with no erase, obtained by the **direct** write path
  because the write-cycle command was unusable on this family, erase reporting as unsupported);
  v1.16 Phase 90, `.planning/v1.16/ledger/PROTOCOL-LEDGER.md:31`.
- **Verdict, booked in advance (D-03):** **`diverges: no comparable baseline`**. The reason is the
  one D-03 itself names for this part — the prior PASS came from a now-forbidden flag, so it is a
  different invocation — and it carries **PD-6's shared clause**: that flag's era semantics also
  skipped the erase, a correction recorded in the procedure's forbidden-invocation table, so the
  baseline was taken under different flag semantics than exist on either arm today. **The control
  arm supplies the baseline**, so `C-08` runs on this position with certainty. Budget it; do not
  discover it.
- **Three structurally-NA steps.** `id` is NA forever on this part — its database entry declares no
  chip-id check and carries a zero id value. `blank-check` is NA because the operation does not
  apply to this family. `erase` is NA because the part does not declare the erase capability. Three
  of the six comparison cells are NA **by construction, not by omission** — record them as NA and
  never as divergences.
- **`known_carried`:** **yes** — FM1608 is the fifth known-carried part, alongside the four SC#5
  names. Two things go in its text.
  1. The folded firmware todo,
     `.planning/todos/pending/fm1608-byte0-write-never-lands-register-cache-elision.md`: a recorded
     defect in which the register cache-skip elides all three shift-register strobes so the first
     byte of a write never lands. **This part gets a full-device write, so if that defect manifests
     the predicted shape is: the write step reports success, and `verify` goes BAD with a
     single-byte mismatch at offset zero and every other byte correct.** If that is what happens,
     the row cites the todo as **known-carried and pre-existing**, and the red does not enter Phase
     165's failure set or Phase 166's findings as a v1.34 discovery. The fix is out of scope in both
     directions — it is pre-existing, so it is also not a v1.33-caused regression.
  2. The recorded blank-check tooling gap on this family from v1.15 Phase 81, which is why the
     step's NA here is expected rather than surprising.
- **The family-label trap, stated once in `anomalies`.** The v1.15 record labels this part's family
  with a value that is its **decimal** algorithm written as though it were hexadecimal; the
  database algorithm is forty decimal, whose hexadecimal form is what the chip schema's family
  column will show. The v1.16 ledger already caught and retired that conflation at
  `.planning/v1.16/ledger/PROTOCOL-LEDGER.md:31`. The two strings differ and **it is not a
  divergence.** Say so once, in this row, and do not repeat it elsewhere.
- **The declared operating voltage, resolved in advance.** Wave 0's
  `bench/cells/CHIP/FM1608-VCC.md` established from source that the database's 3300 mV operating
  voltage for this part is **decorative** — never transmitted to the firmware, with no control path
  on any shield revision, and a generator-side decode gap that is byte-identical on both arms.
  Cite that record in `anomalies`; do not re-derive it, do not measure the socket pin, and record
  socket supply as `not measured — no supply-control path exists in firmware and the shield's rail
  is fixed; v1.34 makes no electrical claim`.
