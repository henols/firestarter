<objective>
Resolve the milestone's single sharpest false-RED risk before arming anything on this target,
then prove the `uno328pb` read chain and its wrong-arm detector.

Purpose: on a **vector** bootloader, avrdude's urclock programmer patches the application's
reset vector and one designated interrupt vector before writing, and applies the same patch
to its own verification. An independent read-back compared against the raw hex would then
report a mismatch at the reset vector on every correctly-flashed board — the independent
check would be the one that lies. Research could not measure which kind of bootloader this
board carries, so the interrogation runs first and the judged span is derived from its
output.

Output: `bench/cells/BRINGUP-uno328pb/` with the interrogation record, the read-back, the
judged verdict and the cross-flash record; plus the resolved judged-span policy written into
`rig-pins.json`.
</objective>
