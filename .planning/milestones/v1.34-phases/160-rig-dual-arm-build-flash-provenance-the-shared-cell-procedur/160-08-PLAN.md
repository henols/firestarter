<objective>
Put the flash read-back on a real device for the first time in this project's history, on the
cheapest of the three chains, and then prove the resulting detector can fail by deliberately
flashing the wrong arm and observing the mismatch (D-03).

Purpose: research is HIGH confidence on every host-side mechanism and LOW confidence on
exactly one thing — the three on-device read chains — because no board was attached and this
avrdude read operation has never been invoked anywhere in this project. The `uno` chain is
proven first so that a failure on either of the other two targets is attributable to that
chain rather than to the reader.

Output: `bench/cells/BRINGUP-uno/` with the probe result, the read-back, the judged verdict,
the cross-flash record, and one bring-up row appended to the canonical evidence record.
</objective>

control arm's `uno` hex to its address extent with the pinned objcopy, compares the

This is the first time this read operation has been invoked anywhere in this project, so
treat its outcome as a measurement rather than a formality, and record what actually
happened including timings. Three outcomes and what each means:

- The read returns exactly 32768 bytes and the judged prefix matches — the chain is proven.
  Record the elapsed time of the read, because plans 09 and 10 need a cost baseline and the
  Leonardo has a bootloader window measured in seconds.
- The read returns a file that is not 32768 bytes — the truncation symptom. Confirm the read
  flag was passed and re-run once; if it persists, this is a finding about the tool or the
  chain and it is a rig failure under the procedure's halt branch, fixed in-phase.
- The read fails to complete or the judged prefix mismatches on a board that was just
  correctly flashed — also a rig failure under the same branch. Do not proceed to task 3:
  a mismatch from a broken reader is not evidence of a working detector. Investigate, record
  the cause, and fix the rig before continuing. If the read direction proves genuinely
  unavailable on this chain, that is SC#2's named-alternative branch and it must be taken
  explicitly, with the alternative check named, its falsifiability shown and its limits
  stated — not by weakening the criterion.

judged span SHA, which it must, since the read spans a bootloader region the hex never
