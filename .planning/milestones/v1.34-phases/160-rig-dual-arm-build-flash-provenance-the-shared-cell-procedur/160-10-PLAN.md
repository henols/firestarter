<objective>
Prove the third and hardest read chain, and complete D-03 across all three targets.

Purpose: this is the milestone's lowest-confidence mechanism. The bootloader must be entered
by a 1200-baud touch, it exits back to the application after a few seconds of inactivity, and
on many boards it enumerates as a different device node than the application does. This
project has recorded one successful multi-second session on this exact chain — a write — but
never a read, and no source establishes that a full read of the whole flash completes inside
the window. So the window is measured, and if it does not fit, SC#2's own escape hatch is
taken deliberately rather than by improvisation.

Output: `bench/cells/BRINGUP-leonardo/` with the window measurement, the read-back or its
named alternative, the judged verdict and the cross-flash record.
</objective>
