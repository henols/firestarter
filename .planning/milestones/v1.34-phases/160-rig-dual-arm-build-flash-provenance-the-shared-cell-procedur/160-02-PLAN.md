<objective>
Build both firmware arms for all three AVR targets from the SHAs pinned in `rig-pins.json`,
commit the six images with a `SHA256SUMS.txt` (D-04), and discharge RIG-01 SC#1's
reproducibility clause by measuring a cold rebuild on every (arm, target) pair rather than
extrapolating from one.

`judge_readback.py` can compare a device read-back against. Their filenames are what makes
the arm visible at the filesystem layer.

Output: `images/` with six arm-tagged `.hex` files, `SHA256SUMS.txt`,
`BUILD-MANIFEST.json`, `REBUILD-CHECK.json`, and `tools/check_rebuild.py`.
