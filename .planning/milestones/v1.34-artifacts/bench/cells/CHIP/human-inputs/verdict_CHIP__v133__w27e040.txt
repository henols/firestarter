**ERROR-IDENTITY CORRECTION -- `Empty input` does not mean what its name says.**
`MSG_ERR_EMPTY_INPUT` (0xA4) has two emit sites in firestarter/src/firestarter.cpp: `:124`, a
genuine empty-buffer check (`data_size == 0`); and `:251-255`, the frame-integrity path, reused
for "CRC mismatch, COBS violation, overflow, or read underrun" specifically BECAUSE a distinct
`MSG_ERR_BAD_FRAME` was deferred (messages.h is codegen-generated from meta's messages.toml; a new
ID needs a TOML catalog update -- deferred, per the comment at that line). The blank-check failure
recorded here is therefore far more likely a TRANSPORT-LAYER frame corruption than a literal empty
input. **Consequence for this row's own erase result: `erase: OK` means the ERASE COMMAND was
accepted, not that erase was independently verified to have cleared the device** -- the step that
would have checked that (blank-check) is precisely the one that died at the transport layer before
returning a verdict. This row does NOT assert the device was actually erased; it asserts only that
the erase command completed and returned OK.
