Say out loud, in the report a human actually reads, what a rail reading does not prove -- and then
close the phase.

rail and asserts no socket-routing bit, so a rig with VPP unhooked still reads a healthy
`vpp_before_mv: 11800`. Today `diagnostic_report.py` contains no rendered sentence of this kind at
all -- research scanned every string literal in the module and found none. This will be the first.

`CTRL_VPP_REGULATOR_ENABLE`, and the `CMD_READ_VPP` branch sets two bits, not one. The substantive
claim survives untouched; the parenthetical does not.

Purpose: a phase that adds a status axis for attributing faults, while leaving the report silent
about the one instrument that looks like it could confirm a rig fault and cannot, has fixed the
vocabulary and left the trap. And a requirement whose own evidence is misquoted is a requirement a
later reader will either over-trust or discard.

Output: a rendered and exported disclosure, a repaired requirement, a measured ATTR-04 record in
MILESTONES.md, six requirement checkboxes flipped, and a sealed phase.
