Take the measurement. This is the phase's hardware-gated half and the milestone's one genuinely
unmeasured number.

At plan time `ls /dev/ttyACM* /dev/ttyUSB*` returned nothing in this container -- no board of either
class is attached, which is exactly why the ROADMAP calls this phase "partially hardware-gated". The
instrument was built and unit-tested in plan `176-04` with no board; this plan attaches two boards

roadmap success criterion 3 requires two distinct artifacts, and an averaged figure would be a
number describing no board that exists. And it must not act on the result: MEAS-01's number gates
PRUNE-08 (Phase 180) and the R4-01 deferral, both explicitly out of scope here. This plan records
the measurement; it does not spend it.

Purpose: replace an assumed per-connect cost with a measured one, per board class.

note, and MEAS-01 marked Complete.

## Flagged planner assumptions

- **PA-14 (the dominant-term hypotheses are hypotheses).** RESEARCH assumptions A1 and A2 -- that
  opening a port on an Uno-class board asserts DTR, resets the ATmega328P into optiboot and pays a
  bootloader wait, and that a Leonardo-class board does not reset on port open -- are training
  knowledge. No in-repo measurement of either exists; that absence is precisely why MEAS-01 was
  written. They MUST be recorded in the artifact as hypotheses the measurement tests, never as
  findings. If the measurement contradicts them, the measurement wins and the requirement is still
  met.
- **PA-15 (the structural floor is a host constant, not a board property).** 2.0 s
  `CONNECTION_STABILIZE_DELAY` plus the 0.5 s `consume_remaining_input` window is paid identically
  on both classes, so the Uno-versus-Leonardo DIFFERENCE rides entirely on what happens inside and
  around those two fixed sleeps. Reporting the floor and the remainder separately is what makes that
  visible. Reducing `CONNECTION_STABILIZE_DELAY` on the strength of this measurement would be a
  separate, riskier change and is out of scope.
- **PA-16 (artifact naming).** `176-MEASUREMENT.md` in the phase directory, following the
  `NNN[-PP]-MEASUREMENT.md` family and the Phase 118 section order. "Two distinct artifacts" is
  satisfied by two clearly separated, never-blended sections in one file rather than two files: the
  Phase 118 precedent is one file per measurement subject, and splitting the file would separate the
  shared provenance block from the numbers it authenticates.
