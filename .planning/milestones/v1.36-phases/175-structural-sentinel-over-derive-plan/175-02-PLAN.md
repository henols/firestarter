over the same corpus and the same production helper plan 175-01 wired: the erase-to-blank-check pairing
(D-05) and the `aq6` UV write-scope ceiling (D-12).

D-05 keeps the erase pairing as its own separately-named leg rather than folding it into the
write-to-verify predicate, because Phase 177 cites this sentinel as its licence and needs a leg that
maps 1:1 onto PRUNE-06's wording. `derive_plan` already relocates the blank-check to sit *after* an
executable erase so it doubles as that step's oracle, single-sourced through `erase_is_executable`, and
that pairing has no guard today.

with no prompt on any path. Three of the four pins are over `derive_plan` output and are easy; the
fourth is the handler-level assertion, and it is the only one that actually guards the ceiling, because
the `derive_plan` pins stay true even if `_resolve_write_scope` is changed to return `"full"` for UV
tomorrow.

Purpose: without D-05 a future edit moving the blank-check back ahead of an executable erase goes
unnoticed; without D-12's handler leg the operator-agreed UV ceiling is unguarded at the only level
that decides it.

Output: seven new legs in the existing sentinel module and one committed evidence transcript.
