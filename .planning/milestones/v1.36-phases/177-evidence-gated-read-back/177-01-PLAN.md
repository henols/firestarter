<assumption_delta_decision>
The `assumption-delta scan` fired `pluralization` on "zero **additional** device reads". Read past
the trigger word, the real delta is genuine and worth recording: `steps[].fingerprint` has meant
"the result of a device read-back" since it existed, and after this plan it has TWO producers --
a measured read-back and a synthesized cheap-path construction.

- **The noun that is now primary:** `Fingerprint.classification` is promoted to mean *the verdict on
  the expected-vs-actual compare, however that compare was established* -- not "the verdict on a
  read-back". The read-back becomes one of two ways to establish it.
- **Decision: `promote`.** The generalized meaning becomes the primary, and provenance is carried
  inside the already-existing `evidence` dict by the honest value `ff_ratio: None` ("not measured"),
  rather than by a new sibling key. A new provenance key was considered by the milestone research
  and is rejected here as unscoped: no requirement ID in Phase 177 covers it, and RPT-A2 in Phase
  181 owns the `steps[].fingerprint` field expansion.
- **What would force a later re-decision:** a consumer that must distinguish measured from
  synthesized to act correctly. Today none exists -- `.claude/skills/devtest-triage/SKILL.md` reads
  `dedup_fingerprint` as a dedup key only and reads neither the classification string nor
  `ladder_state`, and `to_dict()` exports only the classification string.
- **The companion invariant test is ACCEPTED and is Task 2's evidence-key-set leg:** every emitted
  `Fingerprint`, measured or synthesized, carries the identical `evidence` key set. It goes red the
  instant a future phase makes the two shapes distinguishable by absence rather than by value.
</assumption_delta_decision>
