<objective>
Correct the two published pages that became false the moment the operator reversed the authoring
model, and turn `Home.md` from a bullet list of raw source filenames into the real index that
`wiki.py links` walks for reachability. Then run the reachability check against the live clone and
record the green that pairs with plan 168-05’s recorded red.

Purpose: two pages on the front-door repository's wiki are **public and currently false**. One tells
readers not to edit here because their edits will be overwritten by a publish that no longer exists;
the other says the wiki is published from an integration branch, and links to the first for the
explanation. That is a reader-visible defect for every day it goes uncorrected, not end-of-phase
tidying. And per D-22 `Home.md` is load-bearing rather than decorative: under D-06 only `Home.md` counts as
reachability evidence, which is stricter than WIKI-05's "Home or a sidebar" and deliberately so — a
page reachable only from a hand-maintained sidebar is one forgotten edit away from being lost.

Output: a wiki whose own pages describe how it actually works, and a passing reachability check whose
pass means something because the same command was seen to fail two waves earlier.

**This plan writes to a live, public page.** `autonomous: false` is deliberate: auto mode approves
human-verify gates, so the frontmatter flag is what stops an unattended publish.
</objective>
