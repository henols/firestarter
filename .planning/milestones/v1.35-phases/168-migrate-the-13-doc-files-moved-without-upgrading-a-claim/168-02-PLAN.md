<objective>
Execute D-20's retirement inside `wiki.py`: delete `publish`, `sidebar` and `check` outright, keep
`links`, repoint it at a wiki clone, and give it the new hand-maintained-sidebar leg WIKI-05 needs.
Then bring `selftest.sh` down to the surviving cases and add the WIKI-05 negative case.

Purpose: a retired-but-present publish path is a loaded gun aimed at a live public wiki — running
`publish` would wipe the wiki worktree and re-lay it from a source tree this phase deletes. D-20 is
therefore deletion, not dormancy. And WIKI-05's sidebar is hand-maintained from now on, so the check
that guards it must have real work to do rather than comparing generated output against itself.

Output: a `wiki.py` with one subcommand, and a selftest that proves the surviving legs still fail
for the right reasons.
</objective>
