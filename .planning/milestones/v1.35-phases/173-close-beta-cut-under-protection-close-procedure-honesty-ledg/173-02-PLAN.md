## Corrections this plan is built on, stated rather than substituted

`REQUIREMENTS.md:119` says *"`/gsd-complete-milestone` pushes `main` directly today."* That is the
stated reason POLICY-05 exists and **it is false.** Per RESEARCH.md **C-3**, the single `git push` in
the whole `complete-milestone` tree is `steps/git-tag.md:26`, a **tag** push, and all three rulesets
are `target: branch`, so it is ungoverned. `ship.md`'s four pushes are all `${CURRENT_BRANCH}`.

The real failure surface is threefold and worse than a blocked push: a purely local
`git checkout main` + `git merge --squash` at `complete-milestone.md:775` that **succeeds** and
leaves a `main` that can never be pushed with nothing warning; `ship.md:373` opening pull requests
`--base main`; and three consumers CONTEXT.md never names — `execute-phase.md:290`, `quick.md:197`
and `pr-branch.md:28` — which decide what every new phase and quick branch **forks off**, today
`origin/main`, the opposite of the operator's standing branching instruction. D-06's repoint fixes
all three at once, and the third is a pre-existing correctness win this phase gets for free.

CONTEXT.md's criterion-2 row also says POLICY-05 may be satisfied by "a documented admin bypass".
**That branch cannot be written:** `current_user_can_bypass` is `never` on all three rulesets, so no
such bypass exists to document. The note says so rather than inventing one.
