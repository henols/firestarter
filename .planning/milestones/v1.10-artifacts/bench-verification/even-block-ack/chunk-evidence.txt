## ACK-SOURCING PROOF (the core 53-07 claim)
The host's built-in default chunk size for ALL boards is 512 (Phase 55 _calculate_buffer_size returns 512
when the MSG_OK_READY ack is absent). The Leonardo nonetheless transfers in 1024-byte chunks in BOTH
directions. The ONLY way the host arrives at 1024 is by reading the u16 buffer size from the firmware's
MSG_OK_READY ack frame (Phase 55 CAP-01). Therefore the 1024x64 chunking is DIRECT proof of ack-sourced
sizing, distinct from the (now-removed) pre-55 identity-string buffer suffix.
