<objective>
Correct the two cosmetic `electrical.type` labels D-40 names — SST39SF040 `Flash/EEPROM`→`Flash`
and FM1608 `SRAM`→`FRAM` — at the codegen layer, PROVEN label-only (no dispatch / FLAG_CAN_ERASE /
VPP perturbation). The research shows this is NOT label-only as naively written: relabeling

STOP-and-resurface task: HALT and present the collisions to the operator BEFORE editing, then make
only the corrections the operator authorizes as label-only.

perturbing the safety-load-bearing CAN_ERASE / VPP-routing derivation.

pinning assertion, display-layer companions if FRAM is taken, the regenerated chip_database.json,
and a recorded disposition for any part that STOPs.
</objective>
