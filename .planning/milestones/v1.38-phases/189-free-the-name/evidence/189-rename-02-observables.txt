Why three readings, not one (D-03)
-----------------------------------
`git submodule set-url firestarter <url>` rewrites `.gitmodules` alone. It
is `git submodule sync --recursive` that propagates the value into the
superproject's local `.git/config` (`config_url(firestarter)` above) and
into the submodule's own `remote.origin.url` (`remote_origin(firestarter)`
above). A verifier who reads only the file cannot tell whether the sync
step ran; recording all three closes that gap. This is why RENAME-02 is
evidenced across three observables rather than by the file alone.
