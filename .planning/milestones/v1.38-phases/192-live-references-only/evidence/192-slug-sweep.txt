The naive sweep `grep -rn "henols/firestarter" | grep -v
"firestarter_fw\|firestarter_app\|firestarter_prom"` is vacuous here:
`henols/firestarter` is a substring of `henols/firestarter_fw`, so a
line-filtering negative grep under-reports whenever the bare slug and one
of the renamed forms appear on the same line. The pattern below is
boundary-aware instead: it requires the bare slug be followed by a
non-identifier character or the end of the line, so it cannot match
`firestarter_fw`, `firestarter_app` or `firestarter_prom`, all of which
extend the slug with `_`. `/usr/bin/grep` is used throughout because PATH
`grep` in this devcontainer is ugrep and honours `.gitignore`, silently
under-scanning.

A zero bare-slug reading paired with a zero control is a failed gate, not
a pass -- it would mean the pattern itself matched nothing, which is
indistinguishable from a clean tree without the control. Here the control
returns its expected non-zero count (5), so the zero bare-slug reading
above is a genuine clean result, not an artefact of a broken pattern or an
empty scan.

What this sweep deliberately does NOT cover
---------------------------------------------
- Everything under `.planning/milestones/` (D-5). That path is
  historical-by-intent; a sweep proving it untouched is a diff, not a
  grep, and is taken separately as `git status --porcelain -- .planning/milestones/`
  after every task in this plan.
- The record files under `.planning/phases/`, `.planning/notes/`,
  `.planning/seeds/`, `.planning/todos/`, `.planning/debug/` and
  `.planning/v1.3x/`. D-01's rule is: edit only what a reader could act on
  today; everything else in `.planning/` is a record of what the
  repository was called when the record was written, and repairing it
  destroys that evidence.
- `.planning/PROJECT.md`, which is deliberately untouched despite matching
  the bare slug. Two of its lines show why the record/act-on test is the
  right cut: line 1396 carries a live
  `https://github.com/henols/firestarter/actions/runs/30722352902` link
  that now resolves only through the redirect, and line 69 says the
  destructive act in backlog item 999.9 is *claiming* `henols/firestarter`
  -- correct exactly as written. Both are records of history, not live
  references a reader could act on today, so neither is edited.
