<objective>
Make the decoded value an operator can already be harmed by into a sentence an operator can read.

`vdd_mv` is decoded for all 746 rows and read by zero lines of product logic. For 284 of them it
decodes above the 5.0 V the shield can deliver, and today the host says nothing at all. This plan is
the phase's tracer: one part, `FUJITSU/MBM27C1000P,MBM27C1000` — gh#70's part — carried end to end
from the raw database record, through the presenter's existing `raw_config_data` seam, to a field row
and a warning on the operator's screen, with the exit code still 0.

The whole feature is roughly forty lines. The care goes into the sentence, not the mechanism. D-04's
verb was amended by the operator on 2026-09-19 from an assertion about what the part does to an
assertion about what the datum decodes to, because `DECODE-NOTES.md` § 9 — this milestone's own
Phase 198 verdict — established that the nibble selects a programmer rail index rather than a chip
requirement, and only 3 of the 284 rows carry a datasheet-cited value. Every literal in this plan
carries the amended verb.

Purpose: satisfy VCC-01 on the surface the operator checks before committing, and define the
shortfall-statement shape VCC-02 asks for, on the one path that proves the seam works before any
regression pinning is layered on it.
Output: `firestarter/eprom_info.py` carrying a named constant, a testable predicate helper, a gated
injection and two gated output blocks; plus the in-process regression test that pins both the
presence of the warning on an elevated part and its absence on a 5 V one.
</objective>
