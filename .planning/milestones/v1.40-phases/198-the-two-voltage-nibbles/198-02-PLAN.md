<objective>
Complete the VCC decode from upstream, and make the one place it is deliberately not emitted an
explicit, signed decision rather than a silent fallback.

Three vdd indices — 0x06, 0x0D and 0x0E — are absent from the shipped table, so 24 rows currently
reach a fabricated 5000 mV through a `.get` default. Upstream's `xg_vcc_voltages[]` maps all three,
and it is a strict, conflict-free superset of the table already shipped: six shared indices, zero
value conflicts, nine more. Completing it is decoder completion, not correction, so the table stays
in code.

Twelve of those 24 rows then decode to 1800 mV, which is not credible as a program rail for a 5 V
28C-class EEPROM. Those 12 keep 5000, and the exception is written as 12 explicit `UNSOURCED`
override entries rather than as a hole in the table — so that the reason is legible and the honest
statement that nothing backs the 5000 either is on the record.

The remaining 12 rows move: five 1 Mbit-class UV EPROMs to 6000 and seven to 6250, where 6.0 and
6.25 V program VCC is textbook. Combined with plan `198-01`, that is the phase's whole regeneration:
15 rows, 17 field values, `electrical` only.

**Edge-probe accounting for this plan: 3 of the phase's 7 probe-surfaced items.** VOLT-01/adjacency,
VOLT-01/empty and VOLT-01/ordering are all resolved `explicit` and authored as plain
`must_haves.truths` strings above. Plan `198-01` carries 2; plans `198-03` and `198-04` carry the
two `unclassified` items as flagged assumptions.

Purpose: stop 24 rows reporting a number nothing produced, without shipping a number no one believes.
Output: a complete VCC decode table with corrected provenance, 12 signed carve-out entries, and the
phase's full regeneration record.
</objective>
