<objective>
Put the wire field on both sides of the seam, inert, and then make the defect visible.

Two halves, and they must not be reordered. First the field itself: `region-end` on the wire,
`uint32_t region_end` on the handle, reset per command, plus the host constant that names it.
Nothing reads it yet — that is deliberate, and it is what lets the next half happen at all.

Second, the test whose absence is the reason this defect shipped. It seeds a byte non-blank
**outside** the target region, sets a region, and drives `eprom_write_init` with the blank-check
axis live. Against today's whole-device check it must go RED, and it must be **seen** RED with
the transcript captured — a pre-authored gate leg that nobody has watched fail proves nothing.
Its paired negative control, a write into a region that is itself non-blank, stays GREEN
throughout: scoping the check is not deleting it, because a programmed bit on a UV part cannot
be un-programmed.

This plan deliberately ends with one legitimately RED case on the milestone branch. Plan 201-03's
commit greens it. That is the RED→GREEN cycle D-16.1 requires, stated rather than hidden, and no
push to `beta` occurs anywhere in this phase.

Purpose: make the region expressible on the wire and the defect observable in CI.
Output: a parsed-but-unread `region-end` field on both sides, and a RED regression test with its
transcript.
