test can carry.

D-15.3 asks for a source-contract gate. The measured caller census says it must enumerate nine
reference sites, not the four CONTEXT.md names — and six of those nine are function-pointer
assignments, which a gate that counts only direct calls cannot see. That is precisely the shape a
future widening would take: someone repoints `firestarter_operation_main` for one more protocol at
the region form and the relaxation escapes to silicon this phase never benched.

reconstruct: that the two out-of-scope protocols are one `--skip-erase` flag deep rather than
safely latent; which of the two answers the narrowed `uv-write-shortcut` divergence now warrants,
since the todo currently points at a phase that closed; and that the firmware pytest suite's 17
local failures are a stale scan path this phase deliberately did not repair.

rather than remembered.
