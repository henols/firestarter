<objective>
Prove criterion 1 on silicon, on a part that genuinely cannot be erased.

The software is landed and green. What no CI leg can reach is the thing the requirement actually
says: a non-erasable part holding data outside the target region accepts a write into a blank
region. D-14 gates the phase on this and authorises no deferral, because unlike v1.39's PAGE-03
the silicon is on hand and validated.

Two parts, in order. The **W27C512** is electrically erasable, so driving it with `--skip-erase`
reaches the write-init blank check with a non-blank device on the byte-identical code path a UV
part takes — and the whole RED-to-GREEN sequence can be rehearsed as many times as it takes
without consuming anything. Then the **TMS27C512**, once, so the word *non-erasable* in criterion 1
rests on the part rather than on a proxy argument.

Every checkpoint here is a `checkpoint:human-action` carrying `gate="blocking-human"`. Physical
chip seating and chip-ID confirmation are operator work with no CLI, and project experience records
that auto-advance modes auto-approve human-verify gates while `autonomous: false` does nothing to
stop them.

Purpose: turn criterion 1 from a green test suite into a measured fact on non-erasable silicon.
Output: `201-BENCH-RECORD.md`, carrying the RED and GREEN transcripts for both parts, the chip-IDs
as read, the firmware and host versions that produced each, and the labelling that keeps it
evidence rather than a gate.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| operator → socket | A chip seated backwards, or the wrong `512`, meets programming voltage on the wrong pins |
| firmware image → shield → socket | The write path raises VPP and drives program pulses at real voltages on a real part |
| bench record → future reader | A transcript that reads like a test invites someone to believe it re-runs |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-201-06-01 | Spoofing | part identity | critical | mitigate | Both parts are confirmed by chip-ID before any write, never by the package marking. W27C512 is an EEPROM programmed at 12 V and TMS27C512 a UV-EPROM at 13 V; treating one as the other applies the wrong programming voltage to a real device |
| T-201-06-02 | Tampering | high-voltage rails on a mis-seated part | critical | mitigate | The operator states seating and orientation explicitly and confirms any silkscreen-called jumper; Claude does not proceed to any write until the chip-ID read succeeds, which itself requires a correctly seated part |
| T-201-06-03 | Tampering | 12 V on A9 at a derived address | high | accept | Not reachable on either part here: the `mem_size - 64` derivation lives in `eeprom28c_check_chip_id`, on the AT28C protocol path, and neither the W27C512 nor the TMS27C512 dispatches there. It is mitigated by construction phase-wide because `handle->mem_size` was never narrowed |
| T-201-06-04 | Denial of Service | consuming the confirming part | high | mitigate | The TMS27C512 is a UV-EPROM and the confirming write is irreversible. It is deliberately sequenced last, gated on the rehearsal having already gone GREEN twice on an erasable part, and preceded by a read-back that confirms the target region is blank |
| T-201-06-05 | Repudiation | evidence mistaken for a gate | medium | mitigate | D-16.4: an `EVIDENCE` status line in the first ten lines, a closing `How to read this record` section, and an acceptance criterion forbidding the record from describing itself as a test, a gate or a CI leg |
| T-201-06-06 | Spoofing | a GREEN with no RED beside it | high | mitigate | The pre-fix image is built and flashed first and the RED transcript captured on the same part and rig, twice. A GREEN alone cannot distinguish a fix from a device that was blank all along |
| T-201-06-07 | Elevation of Privilege | a publish during a bench session | critical | mitigate | Nothing is pushed. Images are built locally with `pio run`; a branch assertion on all three repositories runs after the pre-fix build, because building an older image is the one step that could leave a detached HEAD behind |
| T-201-06-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | accept | This plan installs no package |
