## OVERRIDE — the identifier ban on section 10 is LIFTED; it stays for the gh#71 comment

The orchestrator's brief told this plan to keep phase numbers, plan ids, `D-NN` decision ids,
`.planning/` paths and backlog numbers out of `DECODE-NOTES.md`. **That instruction was wrong for this
file and is withdrawn.** It was imported from the `VALIDATED-EPROMS.md` rule, whose reason is that the
ledger is read by people who do not have `.planning/`. Two facts, both measured, say it does not
transfer:

- **`tools/` does not ship.** `firestarter_app/pyproject.toml` sets `packages = ["firestarter"]` and
  `MANIFEST.in` lists only `firestarter/` files, `README.md` and `LICENSE`. `DECODE-NOTES.md` is a
  repo-internal developer document, so every reader of it has `.planning/` in the same checkout.
- **The file's own convention is the opposite.** Section 5's heading reads `(Phase 197 D-08 …)`,
  sections 8 and 9 read `(PULSE-01, Phase 197 — …)` and `(VOLT-01, Phase 198 — …)`, their bodies cite
  `D-03`, `D-06`, `D-16` and plan ids `198-01`/`198-03`, and both `Sources:` runs cite
  `.planning/phases/…` paths. Section 6 cites "Plan 86-04".

**So section 10 follows sections 8 and 9 in every respect, with no departure.** Head its parenthetical
the way they head theirs, cite decisions and plans in its body where they carry weight, and give it a
`Sources:` run with `.planning/` paths so a reader gets from the record to the bench evidence in one
hop. The flagged assumption about losing that hop, and the truths and success criteria forbidding the
identifiers, are void. The verify leg has been narrowed accordingly — it no longer greps for those
five patterns.

**Three things this does NOT change:**

1. **The three comment-rule patterns stay in that verify leg.** Section 10 must not argue that it
   exists because a pre-commit check forbids annotating source; that rule was removed on 2026-09-19
   and the record would be stating a false reason for its own existence. Justify the record's home on
   its merits: a one-shield, one-session measurement is provenance for a generated artifact, which is
   exactly what sections 8 and 9 already hold.
2. **The gh#71 comment body keeps its ban, in full.** That is a public GitHub comment on someone
   else's issue, read by people with no repository at all. No backlog number, phase number, plan id,
   decision id or requirement id goes in it. `T-199-33` and the draft-claims verify leg are correct
   as written and are untouched by this override.
3. Phase numbers really are renumbered at milestone close, so a phase-number citation in section 10
   can still rot. Sections 8 and 9 accept that cost; section 10 accepts it too rather than being the
   one section written to a different standard. Prefer a plan id or a `.planning/` path over a bare
   phase number where either will do, since both survive archiving.
