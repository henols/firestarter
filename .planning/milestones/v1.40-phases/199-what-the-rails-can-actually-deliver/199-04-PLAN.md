<objective>
Make the 30-row classification something that can go red.

Thirty rows in the shipped database ask for 18 V or more, eight of them 21 to 25 V, and every one of
them reports as supported — because the generator's ceiling is a regulator's theoretical figure
rather than anything measured at a socket. D-18 says a written table alone is not enough: a
thirty-first row arriving from upstream, or a row changing rails, should redden rather than drift
silently past a reader.

The hard part is that the thing that splits the 30 rows 10 against 20 — the algorithm-to-VPP-path
mapping — lives only in firmware, in a protocol-keyed parameter table the host has no access to.
A test that scanned firmware source would pass in this devcontainer, where the firmware repository
sits as a sibling, and be vacuous in CI, where the host repository is checked out without
submodules. That is a gate that fails open, which is the exact failure mode D-18 exists to prevent.
So the mapping is mirrored as a named constant with its limit stated in the module docstring, and
everything else is derived from the live generated database.

**Edge-probe accounting for this plan: 1 of the phase's 8 probe-surfaced items.** RAIL-02's
`unclassified` probe row stays UNRESOLVED and is surfaced as a flagged assumption in the frontmatter
rather than quietly resolved. The census boundary it points at is nevertheless implemented
concretely here. The other seven are carried by plans `199-02` (2), `199-03` (3) and `199-05` (2).

Purpose: satisfy the classification half of RAIL-02 with a test rather than a claim.
Output: one new test module that reproduces the census from the generated artifact, asserts exact
counts, and has been seen to fail.
</objective>
