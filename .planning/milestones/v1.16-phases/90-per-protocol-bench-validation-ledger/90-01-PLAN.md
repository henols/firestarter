ledger rows are authored. The checker is the automated gate that every subsequent plan's
verify step depends on — it asserts the LEDGER-01 cross-reference contract (join keys resolve,
no SHA duplication), the LEDGER-02 / D-09 structural PASS constraint, and the LEDGER-03

(`diff_db.py` / `check_dispatch.py`) but is a flat assertion set — it deliberately OMITS the
heavy `_classify_diff` root-cause engine.
