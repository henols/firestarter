<objective>
Establish the golden-trace test harness (the shared assert/bless helper) and pin byte-exact register traces for the eprom family — the three write protocols (0x07/0x08/0x0B) plus the eprom chip-id (P4) path.

Purpose: This harness is the foundation every other golden-trace plan (88-02, 88-03) consumes, and the eprom traces are the first leg of the PRIM-01 recompose oracle Phase 89 refactors against. The byte-exact equality compare (count + every element, D-01) is the strongest oracle: any register-write change in Phase 89 trips it.

Output: `_shared/golden_trace.h`, four committed `golden_eprom_*.inc` fixtures, and golden test functions wired into `test_val_eprom.cpp`'s `main()` RUN_TEST list.
</objective>
