<objective>
Make the blank-check step carry the evidence it has always thrown away, and make a host-path report
distinguishable from a firmware-path one — without disturbing a hash that thousands of filed community
issues are keyed on.

Purpose: `check_eprom_blank` already finalises a `CompareResult` on every call and `CompareResult.
fingerprint` is populated by every `finalise()`. The blank-check step discards all of it and attaches
nothing — no `bad`, no `first_offset`, no `first_actual`, which is the exact "which address, what value"
datum the retired firmware `MSG_ERR_NOT_BLANK` used to carry and which the code itself records as the
single most useful datum in a `dev test` failure. Recovering it costs zero extra device I/O.

The difficulty is not the recovery. It is that the obvious place to put it — the existing `fingerprint`
field — is inside `dedup_fingerprint`'s five-entry allow-list, and a passing blank check reads an
all-0xFF device, which `classify_streamed` buckets as `blank/contact` and not `match`. Putting it there
would move all 19 frozen literals and orphan every filed `henols/firestarter_prom` group. So the
evidence goes somewhere the hash does not read, and the *deliberate* distinction DEVTEST-02 asks for
goes in as its own separately-committed, human-confirmed empty-default tag.

Output: a blank-check failure that names the address and the byte, and a report identity that tells the
two comparison paths apart while leaving every already-filed group exactly where it is.
</objective>

## Decisions

- **D-02 (fork F2): the blank-check step's new compare evidence lives in a NEW additive `StepResult` field, never in the existing `fingerprint` slot.** Reason: `dedup_fingerprint` hashes `result.fingerprint.classification` as part of every step's triple. The blank-check step carries no fingerprint today, so its triple ends in an empty classification; attaching one changes the triple for every report on every chip, and it would read `blank/contact` rather than `match` because `classify_streamed` checks the blank/contact bucket first and an all-0xFF read has an `ff_ratio` of 1.0. The rejected alternative — reuse the `fingerprint` field — re-keys all 19 frozen literals and every filed issue group. The chosen shape has a shipped precedent in `_step_dict`'s four `fingerprint_*` siblings, which state the identical argument in-source. Rating: `reversible`.
- **D-03 (fork F4, and RESEARCH Open Question 1): a third empty-default discriminator, `COMPARE_PATH_HOST_TAG = "cmp=host"`, keyed on a new falsy-default `StepResult.compare_path` field, appended to `dedup_fingerprint` after the coverage tag.** Reason: this is the established discipline, twice shipped in the same two files, and its direction is fixed by history — every already-filed report was produced by the firmware comparison path, so the firmware/unknown/legacy side must be the untagged one. A `SCHEMA_VERSION` bump distinguishes nothing because it is not in the hash pre-image; a nonce is already rejected in-source by `generate_inhibited_pattern`'s docstring. **Rating: `one-way`** — once host-path reports are filed under new ids, the `count_agreeing` ladder for that population has restarted and no revert can rejoin them, exactly as gh#20's orphaned id `00e121446ceb` records for the SDP leg. A `checkpoint:decision` therefore precedes the task that implements it.
- **Consequence recorded deliberately (RESEARCH Open Question 1, settled by the DEVTEST-02 requirement text):** the requirement demands the two paths be *distinguishable* AND that no already-filed group be *re-keyed*. The empty default satisfies both simultaneously — filed hashes stay byte-identical because the 19 frozen literals do not move, and new host-path reports form their own groups. That the community N≥2 dedup ladder restarts for the host path is the intended meaning of "distinguishable", not a regression. It is stated here so it is a recorded choice rather than a discovery.
