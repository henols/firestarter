<objective>
Measure what the firmware actually grew, on a cold build of all three AVR targets, then fund it
with new named, SHA-attributed exemptions on both axes and re-arm every tripwire at the new
floor.

Purpose: D-01's stated cost, and it is the tightest constraint in the phase. Measured from the
committed baselines: leonardo's MERGE-05 flash headroom is **0 B** — BASE-01 `flash_used` 26906
plus an allowance of exactly `0 + 96 + 210 = 306` equals the live 27212. RAM headroom is **0 B**
on all three targets: BASE-01 `ram_used` 1573/1579/2014 plus a 2 B tolerance equals the live
1575/1581/2016. So **any** new flash byte on any target needs a third named flash exemption, and
**any** new RAM byte on any target needs a second named RAM exemption — and D-01's cost
paragraph never mentions RAM at all. A single `uint8_t` on `firestarter_handle_t` costs 1 B and
trips it; AVR aligns every scalar to 1 byte, so no padding absorbs it.

**Three figures must not be conflated, and the operative ceiling is not the one the reported
`flash_free` names.** On leonardo:

| figure | value | fails how | guarded? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| MERGE-05 flash band | **0 B** | loudly — `check_size_baseline.py --policy merge05` goes red | **yes** |
| **Caterina-safe growth budget** | **1460 B** | **silently — bricks USB bootloader entry on this board** | **no. deliberately none.** |
| reported physical `flash_free` | 5556 B | — | meaningless as a budget: 4096 B of it *is* Caterina |

Quick task `260820-a7w` did **not** create 4096 B of headroom. It removed the linker's protection
over the bootloader region and relabelled it as free.
`firestarter/platformio.ini:87-94` states the accepted trade in the operator's own terms:
*"report the ATmega32U4's real 32768 B flash size instead of the board JSON's Caterina-reduced
28672 B. Forfeits: 4096 B (Caterina, the USB bootloader). … the linker no longer protects the top
4096 B, so firmware that grows past 28672 B will be linked into and flashed over Caterina,
bricking USB bootloader entry on this board. There is deliberately no compensating guard
(T-a7w-01)."*

So the real leonardo flash ceiling for this phase is **28672 B**, and with live `flash_used`
27212 the safe growth budget is **28672 − 27212 = 1460 B**. That 1460 is exactly the figure
CONTEXT.md D-01 called leonardo's headroom *before* a7w — because pre-a7w `flash_total` was 28672,
so `flash_free` **was** 1460. The number did not move; only its label did.

**A compensating bootloader-safe guard was declined twice** — at a7w (T-a7w-01, "deliberately no
compensating guard") and again in CONTEXT.md §"Deferred Ideas". **Do not plan one.** 1460 B is a
budget to stay inside and to check inline, and crossing it is **unguarded and
unrecoverable-by-CI**: an exemption satisfies the MERGE-05 gate while still bricking the board, so
an exemption is not the remedy. If this phase's firmware growth would cross 28672 B on leonardo,
that is a **STOP-and-report**, not something to absorb.

Output: a cold-measurement transcript, two new exemptions, a re-recorded live baseline, and a
severed `*_v151*` tripwire family.

**Requirement flips owned by this plan: none.** It advances LOCK-02.
</objective>
