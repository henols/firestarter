<objective>
Land the hand-curated family-level protection table (LOCK-01) as a new pure Python module
shaped like `sdp_capability.py`: literal collections of string literals with per-row
provenance comments, importable with no loader.

Purpose: LOCK-01 and D-05. `infoic.xml` cannot supply protection readability — W29C020C, a
readable permanent boot block, is flag-identical to W29EE011, SDP-only and unreadable, and
the whole AMD readable group carries zero protection bits. That measured negative is what
licenses hand-curation under DATA-04's proof rule rather than making it a violation of it.
The curation surface is measured and finite: **217 DB entries across algorithms `0x05` (27
entries) and `0x06` (190 entries), carrying 273 distinct alias tokens**. Everything else —
529 of 746 rows — is algorithm-derivable and needs no curated token at all.

Output: `firestarter_app/firestarter/protection_readability.py` carrying the readability,
mechanism and permanence axes plus the C-17 ambiguity record; and
`firestarter_app/tests/test_protection_table_citations.py` proving every citation resolves.

**Requirement flips owned by this plan: none.** It advances LOCK-01; the flip belongs to
`151-09`, which lands the AST gate that is the other half of LOCK-01's proof.
</objective>
