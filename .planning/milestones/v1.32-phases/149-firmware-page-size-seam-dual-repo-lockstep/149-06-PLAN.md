<objective>
Measure what the firmware seam actually cost, and fund it in one visible, attributable place.

The budget position is exact and stark. BASE-01 leonardo is 26906; the pre-edit tree measured 27002;
the leonardo band is **0 B** and `MERGE05_DEFECT_FIX_EXEMPTION_BYTES = 96` is **already fully
consumed** by Phase 145's W27C512 fix. So leonardo's remaining MERGE-05 flash headroom before this
phase is **exactly 0 bytes**, and uno-class has 64 B. The band admits nothing. D-12's answer is a
**new, separately named, SHA-attributed** exemption that mirrors the existing mechanism without
touching it — not a re-anchored BASE-01 (that is what made MERGE-05 green at Phase 144, and PGSZ-04
exists precisely to stop it happening a third time), not a widened band, and not a red gate shipped
with an explanation.

This plan is strictly downstream of plan 04 because the exemption's **value cannot be known until the
firmware edit is built and cold-measured**. It could not have been pre-authored, and a placeholder
would have made the planted fixtures wrong and the RED/GREEN transcripts meaningless.

One thing the research did not surface and this plan must handle: `--policy merge05` requires
`ram_used` **exactly unchanged**, and the exemption D-12 authorises is a flash allowance that
deliberately does not touch that clause. Plan 04 added a `uint16_t` field to a file-scope global
handle, so `.bss` is expected to grow. Predicted RAM delta: **+2 B on all three targets**. Measure it;
if RAM moved at all, it needs its own separately named exemption for the same reason flash does.

Purpose: make PGSZ-04's flash and RAM deltas measured, attributable and admitted rather than absorbed.
Output: three cold transcripts, the exemption mechanism, five repaired test legs, three re-planted
fixtures, and the post-change section of `149-PAGE-SIZE.md`. The change is **software-proven and
unvalidated on silicon**.
</objective>

- **PLANNER FINDING, beyond `149-RESEARCH.md` §R8(d): the RAM clause has ZERO tolerance and this phase

  Consequences the research's blast-radius list does not include:
    * a **second** separately-named exemption, for RAM, is required — same mechanism, same

    * there is a **fifth** broken test leg, `test_policy_merge05_fires_on_ram_move`, and a **third**
