<objective>
Land the host side of the seam: the provenance-keyed emit rule that decides **which** chips get a
delivered page size, the exhaustive invariant that proves the rule held, and the committed wire-delta
list that keeps Phase 148's golden intact while making this change's blast radius reviewable data.

The rule is a claim about **provenance, never about a part**: `page_size` is meaningful for the
algorithm that consumes it, and a record filed upstream under `0x07` or `0x0B` is not evidence about
a 28C page buffer. Measured, 66 of the 84 `algorithm: 13` rows are promotions by
`classify()` arm 2, not upstream-native `0x0D` records. Only 18 qualify — 15 at page 128 and 3
already at 64. This is the Phase 148 D-01 category error one field over, and the two rows the rule
most visibly excludes are a Cypress and a Fujitsu **FRAM**, parts with no page buffer at all.

Per D-02 the host needs **zero runtime code change**: `database.py:417-419` already lifts
`programming.page_size` into `_map_data` and `:552-553` already emits wire `page-size`, and
`page-size` is already the 6th of the wire golden's 9 keys. The work here is the emit rule, the
tests, and three neighbourhoods of stale comments.

Purpose: make PGSZ-01's "travels from `chip_database.json` over the wire" literally true for exactly
the corroborated rows, with the selection rule proven exhaustively host-side where the assertion can
be total and free.
Output: a regenerated database, a new invariant suite, a committed 18-delta fixture, and the DB-side
section of `149-PAGE-SIZE.md`. The change is **software-proven and unvalidated on silicon**.
</objective>
