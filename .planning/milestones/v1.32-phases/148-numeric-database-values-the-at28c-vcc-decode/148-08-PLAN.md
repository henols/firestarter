Make the deletions permanent, complete the review artifact, and state the breaking change to the
user.

lookup table keyed on part number and no new sibling to `_PAGE_SIZE_BY_PART`. Both are properties
that decay silently unless something asserts them — three such guess tables were deliberately
deleted in Phase 70 and the discipline exists precisely because the pattern comes back under a new
name.

investigator looks; not split across plan SUMMARYs. It carries the `diff_db.py` output, the 56-chip
mover list, the D-03 justification with its citation, and the **explicit non-claim** that the
`vcc == 5500` group was deliberately left untouched.

**D-17**: nothing in the CLI explains the change — the output simply becomes correct. The reasoning
lives in `148-DB-DIFF.md`, and the user-facing statement is a changelog entry covering both the
AT28C VCC correction and the **breaking** numeric schema (stale string-schema
`~/.firestarter/database.json` overrides no longer load, per **D-10**).

Purpose: close DATA-03 and DATA-04 with assertions rather than assurances, and leave the phase
reviewable.
