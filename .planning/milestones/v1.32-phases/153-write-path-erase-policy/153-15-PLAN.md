<objective>
Re-plant the size tripwire on a new `*_v153*` fixture family so the widened allowance is still a
ceiling, and record the severance.

Purpose: ERASE-08's last clause. Plan 14 widened the MERGE-05 flash allowance by an adjudicated
amount. An allowance that no test can exceed is not a ceiling — it is a blank cheque. Every leg that
asserted at the old allowance must be re-derived from the new one, and every planted fixture must be
seen to fail.

Output: thirteen new fixtures, eight repointed legs, one strengthened leg, and a severance record in
the module docstring.

**Requirement flips owned by this plan: none.** It completes ERASE-08, which plan 14 flipped; this
plan is what makes that flip defensible.

Cites `D-153-01` (the funding posture) and plan 14's measured figures.
Locked decisions honoured: **L-01** (the tripwire fixtures are re-planted on a new `*_v153*` family
and the `*_v151*` family is never edited) and **L-10** (the severance record carries the literal
honesty wording).
</objective>
