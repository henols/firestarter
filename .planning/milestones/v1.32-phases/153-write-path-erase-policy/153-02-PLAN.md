<objective>
Remove the pre-write blank check from protocol `0x0D`, and prove it with a case that was observed
failing first.

Purpose: ERASE-01 and 152-CONTEXT.md D-07. On `0x0D` the silicon auto-erases per page during the
write and the write path already read-back-verifies each page, so a pre-write blank check is not a
safety net — it is a false precondition that makes a non-blank AT28C part un-writable without a
flag. That is the `Not blank, at 0x000000` failure pasted on gh#20, and `chip_test.py` calls
`write_eprom` with no flags, so `dev test` hits it on any non-blank part.

Output: one new native case, then a three-line deletion. Nothing else in the handler moves.

**Requirement flips owned by this plan: ERASE-01.**

Cites `D-153-05` (no `FLAG_CAN_ERASE`-gated erase block may be added to `eeprom28c_write_init` in
its place).
</objective>
