<objective>
Give protocol `0x0D` a real, standalone chip erase: one `case CMD_ERASE:` arm and one operation
body implementing the **software** six-byte sequence, at 0 B RAM.

Purpose: ERASE-03's firmware half and ERASE-04. Today `firestarter erase AT28C256` is refused —
the host clears the capability flag and the handler has no arm — while `firestarter info` says the
part is electrically erasable. That contradiction is on gh#20's 2026-08-07 paste. Phase 121 D-12
closed it by making the host claim less; this phase closes it by making the firmware do more.

Output: one new constant, one forward declaration, one operation body, one switch arm and one
corrected comment. No new table, no new message id, no new control-register write.

**Requirement flips owned by this plan: none yet** — ERASE-03 and ERASE-04 flip once plan 04's
inversions and plan 05's negative source scan land. This plan advances both.

Cites `D-153-01` (supply form), `D-153-02` (SDP-disable prefix), `D-153-03` (GATE-03 mechanism
correction), `D-153-04` (no post-erase blank check; sector address ignored) and `D-153-05` (erase
stays out of the write path).
Locked decisions honoured: **L-02** (the erase body is RAM-neutral by construction — inline
writes, no new `.data` table) and **L-09** (the SDP-disable-prefix question, settled by plan 01 and
implemented here).
</objective>
