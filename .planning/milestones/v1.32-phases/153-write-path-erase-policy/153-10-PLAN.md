<objective>
Absorb the `dev test` plan-shape change that restoring the capability flag produces on all 84
algorithm-13 rows, and correct the two reason strings that assert the family has no erase.

Purpose: ERASE-03's `dev test` half. This reads like a reason-string edit and is not: three coupled
sites in `chip_test.py` decide whether the erase step exists, whether it is executable, and where
blank-check sits. Restoring the flag flips all 84 rows from an NA erase with a pre-write blank check
to a supported destructive erase with the blank check moved after it, where it doubles as the
erase's oracle. That is the intended and better shape — and it has to be funded, not discovered.

Output: two corrected reason texts, one defensive arm kept with a reachability test, and four
re-measured legs whose figures come from the live plan.

pattern mapper's finding that the `_PROTOCOL_EEPROM_28C` reason arm becomes unreachable rather than
merely stale, whose delete-versus-keep disposition this plan states.
