## Framing that must survive into the code comments

- **Phase 116's D-01 was optimistic and this plan says so out loud.** The RED→GREEN flip is **four**
  edits, not one line: the `test_filter` line, the `set_data` un-mock, the five response-code
  assertions, and one new regression case. Silently editing the oracle to make a later fix pass is
  precisely the failure mode this milestone's harness-before-fix ordering invariant exists to
  prevent.
- **CORRECTION 4 is load-bearing for prose: 66 of 84, never "all 84".** `DIP24_2816`'s 19 chips are
  inhibited on the `0x2AAA` loads, **not** the `0x5555` loads (`0x2A` has bit 3 set, `0x55` does
  not). `DIP32_28C512_EEPROM`'s 18 chips are inhibited **0 of 6** from a fresh boot and are only
  hazardous under a stale upper-address bit. Any sentence saying "the `0x5555` writes are inhibited"
  is wrong for 19 chips.
