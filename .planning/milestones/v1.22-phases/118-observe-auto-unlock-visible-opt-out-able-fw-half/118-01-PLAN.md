Rewrite `firestarter_app/tools/check_no_log_in_sdp_window.py` so its scanned window is the two
function bodies where the SDP inter-byte timing window actually lives, re-plant the committed
anti-hollow fixture inside the new window, and repair every pytest case the redefinition breaks —

`eeprom28c_emit_command_sequence`, which is where the real inter-byte window is. Phase 118-04 adds

it would either block legitimate work or (worse) be quietly deleted. This is the phase's sharpest
hazard and it was foreseen: rewriting the window silently hollows the gate the rewrite is meant to
strengthen, and turns its own anti-hollow pytest RED.

Output: a gate whose PASS means what OBS-03 says it means, with its planted-violation proof intact.
