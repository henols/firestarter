<objective>
XACT-01 / SC1: Prove the hardened COBS transport is byte-exact on a clean board. Operator-witnessed.

N=5 consecutive framed READ transfers byte-identical on a clean Uno (512 B buffer) and a clean Leonardo
(1024 B buffer), plus N=5 WRITE->read-back->compare cycles per board with independent host-side SHA-256
compare. Strong form: if the original W27C512 chip is on the bench, the read runs also hash-match the
GATE-1.8d Rev 2.0 Leonardo baseline at
`.planning/v1.6/consistency-check-runs/W27C512-leonardo-20260526-155617-v2-rev20/` (canonical run_01.bin
SHA prefix `19710f6e...`). Else self-consistency alone satisfies SC1 and the artifact states so (D-05).

This phase adds NO transport behavior. It exercises the merged hardened firmware + host harness
(53-02) on real hardware. The clean-board proof targets shield Rev 2.0 (D-07) — but the operator MUST
confirm the actual silkscreen rev at session time because the EEPROM hw_revision byte cannot distinguish
revs.

Purpose: tie the byte-identity claim back to the known-clean, already-baselined Rev 2.0 Leonardo lineage.
Output: per-run + per-cycle binaries and SHA tables under .planning/v1.10/bench-verification/.
</objective>
