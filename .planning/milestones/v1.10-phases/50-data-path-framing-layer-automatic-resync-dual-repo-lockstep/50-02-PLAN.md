<objective>
Rewrite the firmware data-block transport in `rurp_serial_utils.cpp` to the Phase-49-frozen
`[COBS(payload+CRC8)][0x00]` framing: `rurp_communication_read_data()` becomes a streaming COBS
decode-in-place into `data_buffer` with CRC8 verify and drain-to-`0x00` resync, and the dormant
`rurp_communication_write()` becomes its COBS streaming-encode mirror. Removes the literal 2 s
`timeout_ms` loop (the SC1 cascade source). Greens the Wave-0 firmware Unity suite from Plan 01.

Purpose: kills the `len_u16`-corruption → wrong-length-read → 2 s-timeout cascade by making the
`0x00` delimiter (not a length field) the frame boundary, and bounds desync to one frame. The
drain-to-`0x00` + immediate-`OP_MSG_ERROR` behavior is D-01 (resync + fail-fast, no block-level
retransmit/ACK); the `#` marker is kept and only the post-`#` bytes change (D-04).

Output: a streaming firmware encoder/decoder (no second ~512 B buffer), CRC8 reused unchanged,
and the `case '#'` error surface preserved. Reads stay on the frozen `MSG_DATA_CHUNK` frame.
</objective>
