drift fails a test instead of shipping silently.

queued-byte mock helper, and a scripted Uno RAM-ceiling assertion. All RED at end of this plan
(implementation lands in Wave 2).

decoder case; SC2 asserts bounded recovery, not mere detection) and the test-side of D-06 Option A
(CRC8 reused unchanged per D-05; reads stay on `MSG_DATA_CHUNK`).
