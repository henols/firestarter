<objective>
Build the corpus measuring instrument this phase's sweeps and their acceptance
criteria both depend on, and write down every gate disposition — the 8 app-repo
cross-repo paths (D-05), the 22 firmware-repo fail-open gates (research F4,
Ruling D), and the blob-sha exemptions (research F3, Ruling B).

Purpose: the sweep's own definition of "done" is a hit count, and D-01's triage
procedure is stated per hit. Without a committed, re-runnable measuring instrument
every later criterion degrades into an assertion. Equally: source-scanning gates in
this project **fail open** — research proved `test_sdp_table_parity.py` reports
`5 passed` while the real `EEPROM_SDP_ENABLE` terminal byte is corrupted from `0xA0`
(SDP lock) to `0x10` (chip erase). Silence about a fail-open gate is the one bad
option, so every gate this sweep can reach is either controlled (plan 03) or
recorded here as a named exposure with its cause.

Output: `survey_provenance.py`, `sweep-corpus-baseline.md`,
`sweep-gate-dispositions.md`.
