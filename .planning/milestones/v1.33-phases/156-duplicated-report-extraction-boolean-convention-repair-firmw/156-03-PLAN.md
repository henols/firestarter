<objective>
Replace the four byte-identical VPP-mismatch packing blocks with one shared helper, preserving
the AVR arithmetic exactly, and re-derive the committed branch-inventory golden inside the same
commit.

Purpose: those four blocks held 24 of the image's 31 `__udivmodhi4` call sites between them.
Two live in `eprom_check_vpp`; two live in `static void flash_intel_check_vpp` -- NOT in
`flash_intel_write_init`, which is where criterion 1 and DEDUP-01 both say to look (C-1: the
symbol-table attribution and the lexical location are two different true facts, because
`flash_intel_check_vpp` is static and fully inlined). The hard part is not the extraction. It is
that `(vpp_mv + 50)` promotes to a 16-bit `unsigned int` on AVR, so a helper whose parameters are
`uint32_t` instead of `uint16_t` compiles to `__udivmodsi4`, erases the whole saving, and changes
the wrap point above 65485 mV -- while every native test still passes, because native `int` is
32-bit and cannot see it.

Output: `mem_util_report_voltage` in `src/proms/memory.cpp` with its declaration in
`include/memory_utils.h`, four call sites collapsed to two-ternary form, and
`tests/golden/protocol_branch_inventory.json` re-derived from 23 sites to 22 -- all in one
commit, measured on all three AVR targets.
</objective>
