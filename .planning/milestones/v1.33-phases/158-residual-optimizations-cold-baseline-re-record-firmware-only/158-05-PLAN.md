## The decisions this plan executes

- **OD-3 -- LAND-03 is FIXED, not carried**, on the axis-split argument. BASE-01 has three axes, not one: a **growth** axis (`flash_used`, `ram_used`) that is frozen and is what the not-re-anchored leg exists to pin; a **board-identity** axis (`flash_total`) already licensed to move with cause by an earlier quick task, with the split recorded in that leg's own docstring; and a **test-inventory** axis (`native_envs` case and succeeded counts) which is what this plan moves.
