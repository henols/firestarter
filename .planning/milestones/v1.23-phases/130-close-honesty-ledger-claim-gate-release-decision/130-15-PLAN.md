<objective>
Verify both distribution channels are actually public, after the operator has acted — and fail visibly if they
have not.

Three constraints converge here, and each exists because of a specific past failure:
- **CONSTRAINT 5** — the tag is read, never computed. b15 is deterministically derivable, and that is exactly why
  the constraint is not relaxed: a concurrent cut, an out-of-band tag or a rehearsal-tag collision would change it.
- **CONSTRAINT 8 / D-03** — `beta-build.yml`'s ARM steps are `continue-on-error: true` by design, so b15 can
  publish perfectly green **with no py32 asset at all**. REL-02's only prior evidence is a rehearsal draft that was
  deleted after the run, so this is the one chance to prove it on a real published release.
- **CONSTRAINT 7** — PyPI resolution is checked directly. Six of thirteen published app betas never reached PyPI;
  `3.0.0b12`'s absence is the corroborating case, and `publish.yml`'s own comment records the cause: a release
  created by another workflow using a PAT lacking `workflow` scope suppresses the `release.published` event.

first task stops with a clear message. That is the designed behaviour: an auto-approved continuation must fail
closed, not attempt the cut.
