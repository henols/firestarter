`<!-- recordscan:history ... -->` or `<!-- recordscan:allow ... -->` anywhere on the hit line, with

true when written and is preserved deliberately; `allow` for the self-reference case where the line
*defines* the needle. Both are invisible in rendered markdown, which is why they are markers rather
than prose. Require at least one non-whitespace character of free text after the keyword and treat a
bare marker as **not** matching — an exemption with no stated reason is the fail-open shape this
milestone keeps finding, and the reason text is what a future reader audits.
