the geometry record precedes this edit in commit history, and that the reserved map has the

turns the ordering into `git rev-list --is-ancestor` rather than a claim. The addresses written here
are what Phase 129's PCB record will quote, so they are effectively permanent.

a zero-length `BOOTLOADER` seam, four `PROVIDE`d symbols and two linker `ASSERT`s; plus
`tests/test_flash_geometry_recorded_before_linker.py` and `tests/test_py32_flash_map.py`.
