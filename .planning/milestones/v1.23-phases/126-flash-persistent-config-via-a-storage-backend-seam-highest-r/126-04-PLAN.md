<objective>
Measure AVR flash and RAM for all three targets on cold builds immediately after the AVR move and
before any ARM work, compare them under both named comparators against their named baseline files,
and record the delta with its cause — re-baselining the live baseline in its own justified commit
only if a delta fires.

Purpose: this phase is the only one in v1.23 that edits a file compiled into all three AVR targets.
`scripts/check_size_baseline.py`'s strict-equality comparator is already armed, Leonardo's A-5
allowance is **zero growth** against 2656 B of headroom, and splitting one translation unit into two
while adding a `bool` return can move bytes even with link-time optimisation and section garbage
collection. This is the only point in the phase at which a delta is attributable to the policy split
alone.

Output: three cold AVR build logs with their measured flash and RAM figures, both comparators' exit
codes and verbatim PASS/FAIL lines with their baseline files named, the three warning-gate results,
and either a recorded 0 B result with its mechanism or a re-baseline commit whose message states why
the bytes are legitimate.
</objective>
