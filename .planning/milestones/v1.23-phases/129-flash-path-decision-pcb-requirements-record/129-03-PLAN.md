**`### 1.3 SWD, and why PA13/PA14 are not free pins [CITED: PY32F071 Datasheet Rev 0.7 EN p.44 note 2]`**
PA13 is `SWDIO` and PA14 is `SWCLK`, configured as those alternate functions after reset, the
former with an internal pull-up and the latter with an internal pull-down. Both also carry other
alternate functions (name them), so reassigning them is possible and would foreclose the
last-resort recovery path. `nRST` is a dedicated pin with an internal weak pull-up. A usable
header needs SWDIO, SWCLK, GND, a VDD sense and — given §1.2's option-byte hazard — `nRST`.
