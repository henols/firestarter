  AT28C04/AT28C16 family by chip name and routes them to `support_status: adapter-required` with a

  on both axes. `support_status` stays `protocol-not-implemented`. NO firmware handler is committed (D-01).

the v1.13-close invariant that NOTHING graduates to `supported` and both DB gates stay green.
