<objective>
Delete the 12 V bulk-erase routine that stood one host-policy line away from a 5 V-only part, and replace
the assertion that guarded it with one that still means something afterwards.

Purpose: SAFE-08. `configure_flash_5v_page`'s `CMD_ERASE` arm is unreachable while the host clears
`FLAG_CAN_ERASE` for every `0x05` part, and D-12 removes all of it rather than leaving the hazard
standing. D-13 requires the unreachability to be stated precisely. D-17 requires the tests that go
tautological to be adjudicated HERE, in the phase whose own edit makes them tautological, not left for a
later sweep.

This plan is expansion work on the firmware side of the phase; the phase's tracer slice is `183-03`
Task 1. It depends on `183-01` for the milestone branch and for the pre-deletion baseline figures the
shrink is measured against.

Output: a `flash_5v_page.cpp` with no 12 V erase path, and a `test_val_5v_page.cpp` that proves the
resulting safety property rather than asserting a branch that no longer exists.
</objective>
