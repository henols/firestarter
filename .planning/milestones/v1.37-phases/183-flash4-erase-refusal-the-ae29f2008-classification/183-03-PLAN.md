<objective>
Give the operator a one-line refusal for `firestarter erase` on a flash4 (`0x05`) part, fired before
anything connects, in its own policy module.

Purpose: SAFE-06. gh#62's reporter saw `Connecting... OK` followed by a bare `Not supported`, read it as
a malfunction, and routed around it with `--force` under another chip's identity. D-02 puts the refusal
before the port opens. D-03 puts its reasoning in a third sibling to `sdp_capability.py` and
`jp5_gate.py`. D-07 fixes the message at one line naming the part and nothing more — the cause and the
alternative move to Phase 187's REPLY-03 (D-09), and the `--force` warning is REPLY-03's alone (D-10).
D-06 keeps the default exit code at 1 and puts the no-op exit 0 behind an explicit flag.

This plan depends on `183-01` because SAFE-07 and activation decision D-3 require the mechanism to be
priced before it is chosen. Task 1 is this phase's TRACER: one path, wired end to end from the operator's
argv through the policy module to the exit code, verified by a real end-to-end command before any
expansion task runs.

Output: `flash4_erase_gate.py`, its test file, and the `erase` handler wiring.
</objective>
