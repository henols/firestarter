<objective>
Fix the chip-to-pin-map assignment at the generator, and retire the false parity claim that stood
where the fix belongs.

Purpose: `resolve_pinout_key` abandons infoic's `variant_lo` field only at `pin_count == 32`,
substituting a hand-tuned size threshold. That is the one place the generator breaks its own
"no per-IC names, no per-family tables" contract, and it is exactly why all eight 1 MB rows landed
on `DIP32_STD` with `vpp-pin: [1]`. The fix is rule-shaped and its blast radius was measured over all
767 filtered infoic rows: exactly 8 rows change key.

Output: the `variant_lo` forks in the 32-pin arm; a module-local decode boundary with an honest name;
`MAX_27C020_SIZE` and its self-comparing parity arm deleted; a real 32-pin dispatch test in its
place; and `DECODE-NOTES.md` section 1 brought current.
