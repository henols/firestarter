records outlived the trees they described, and clearing a failure without recording its cause would be that

deleted, no run on `main` can exist at all, so both are unsatisfiable as written — and unreachable for a
second independent reason, since `main` is protected in all three repositories and nothing in this phase
lands there. Amending them in the same phase as the work, with the conflict stated before the choice, is
this project's established practice.

tracking documents amended by hand with the reason on the record.
