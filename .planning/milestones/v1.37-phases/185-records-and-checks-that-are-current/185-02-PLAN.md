<objective>
Prove success criterion 1 independently — a fresh cold rebuild, not the capture the record was
transcribed from — and settle the disposition of the six D-12 orphan-candidate fixtures by measurement.

Purpose: Plan 185-01's gate run is fed the very logs the baseline was transcribed from, so it can only
prove the transcription was internally consistent. Criterion 1 asks for something stronger: that a build
run now, from clean, produces the figures the record claims. `--rebuild` is the only mode that does that,
and it is the one this project's CI never runs — the cold rebuild is a local-run obligation.

Second, D-12's premise needs re-measuring before it is acted on. D-12 was stated on a measurement that
searched for six expanded filenames and found them referenced nowhere. Prose in this repository cites
fixture families in collapsed brace form. D-12's own stated criterion — that a prose citation in the
checker's docstring counts as a live reference and its subject is kept, as every other retired generation
was — decides the outcome once the search matches the form the prose actually uses.

Output: a backgrounded `--rebuild` transcript with its exit code, and a recorded, evidenced disposition
for the six candidates.
</objective>
