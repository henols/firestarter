## 1. What the milestone set out to do, and what it did

The milestone goal was to stop the project withholding what it already knew — from the operator about
to destroy a chip, from the reporter who had waited a month, and from the maintainer reading a guard
that no longer existed.

Three signals arrived inside a week and were the same defect wearing three coats. A user **destroyed
chips** (gh#60) for want of a warning the repository could already have given: the hardware fact had
been in-tree since 2026-07-10 in `notes/jumper-display-ground-truth.md` — JP5 is `A19_CUT`, a bridged
solder jumper, and JP4 routes VPP to socket pin 1. A user was **taught to bypass a safety gate**
(gh#62): `erase AE29F2008` refused with a bare `Not supported` — a *correct* refusal, since flash4
clears `FLAG_CAN_ERASE` so a 12 V bulk erase cannot reach a 5 V-only part — and the reporter, told
nothing, re-ran under a different chip's identity with `--force`. And **three reporters were still
waiting**: v1.36 shipped the machinery answering gh#23, #28 and #31 and, per its own scope note, did
not work the tracker.

All three are closed, and the internal half with them: a file naming a deleted guard, a baseline
three milestones stale, two tests asserting coverage that no longer existed.

## 6. A verification defect found at close, and its real cause

`188-VERIFICATION.md`'s `covered_digest` matched the files it covered at neither the verification
commit nor HEAD, so `verification.status` returned `stale` unconditionally and would have blocked
phase transition permanently.

**The first diagnosis — that the digest was hand-written — was wrong, and is retracted.** Sweeping
historical revisions of the covered files showed the declared value is exactly the digest of the
declared 26 files with `.planning/ROADMAP.md` at revision `cccfb7aa`: an honest computation, taken
before the phase's own close amendments rewrote ROADMAP.md and before the report was committed.

**It is systemic.** The same reconstruction explains 183 (ROADMAP at `dc309bf8`), 184 (ROADMAP at
`25304374`) and 187 (REQUIREMENTS at `aa17f0a3`). **Five of this milestone's seven phases committed a
digest that could never verify**, because the fingerprint is taken before the close sequence's own
ROADMAP/REQUIREMENTS edits land. 186 is unexplained by a single-file sweep and may involve more than
one covered file moving.

Only 188's digest was re-anchored, because that phase's content was independently re-verified during
its UAT. **182–187 were deliberately left stale**: re-anchoring them would assert a check nobody
performed. Their reports all read `status: passed` on their own evidence; the `stale` flag is a
record-gate artifact, not a claim that the work is unverified.

Two measurement traps that made the first pass wrong, both now documented: submodule files are not in
meta's tree and must be read via the gitlink from inside the sub-repo, and `covered_files` appears in
both JSON-array and YAML-block-list shapes — a parser matching only the former reports "legacy, no
fingerprint" and hides the problem entirely.

- **STATE.md Roadmap Summary** — already stale, still reading "Phases: 6 (182–187)" and "28
  requirements" since 188 was added on 2026-09-12.

All three were repaired against a pre-write snapshot and reconciled with `query progress`, which
recomputes from disk and read 7/7, 49/49, `percent: 100`. The lesson is the procedure: snapshot
ROADMAP.md and STATE.md before `phase.complete`, diff after, and trust the recomputed oracle over the
writer's own report.
