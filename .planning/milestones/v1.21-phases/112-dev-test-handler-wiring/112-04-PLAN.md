prompts are too complicated and partly broken (choice strings containing `/`
collide with `rich`'s `/` choice separator, so natural inputs like `new`/`used`/`2.0`
are rejected), and every question the tool asks is either firmware-queryable
(shield/hw/fw) or self-reported-and-unverifiable (chip origin, UV eraser).

`112-UAT.md`), DESCOPE the entire interactive provenance-collection model:
delete the four prompts and the `Provenance` human-input fields, replace them with
firmware/DB auto-capture (`hw_revision`, `fw_board_identity`, `protocol`), and
redefine `is_submittable` on auto-captured completeness only.

This REVERSES RPT-04 and decisions D-04 / D-05 / D-06 (operator-approved descope).
The `--destructive` TTY safety confirm (SAFE-03) is NOT provenance and MUST stay.

SC2 bench re-verify (which was blocked by the broken prompts).

value-returning hardware-revision auto-capture, and updated/rewritten tests.
