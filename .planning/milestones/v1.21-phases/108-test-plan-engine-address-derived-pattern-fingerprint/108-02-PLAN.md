<objective>
Create the greenfield `chip_test.py` module seeded with its pure, bench-free compute layer:
the address-derived pattern generator (D-01/D-02 / PATT-01) and the four-bucket byte-mismatch
fingerprint classifier (D-03/D-04 / PATT-02). These are the coupled pattern+diagnosis pair the
phase mandates ship together, and they have no analog — they are new pure functions unit-tested
with hand-built byte arrays. The N-run divergence math they lean on is REUSED from
`consistency_check_eprom`, never reimplemented (D-04 mandate).

Purpose: A write/verify pattern that actually exposes stuck/shorted/aliased address lines
(instead of hiding them like a fixed pattern would) plus an honest classifier that emits
`indeterminate` rather than over-confidently mis-diagnosing — grounded in this project's own
false-PASS history.
Output: `chip_test.py` with `address_fold_byte`, `generate_pattern`, `prepass_images`, a shared
byte-diff-offset helper, `classify_fingerprint`, a `Fingerprint` record, and the four outcome
label strings; `tests/test_chip_test.py` covering PATT-01/02.
</objective>
