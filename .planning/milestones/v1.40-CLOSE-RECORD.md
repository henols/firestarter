## 2. What the milestone set out to do, and what it did

Every programming parameter the host sends is either what `infoic.xml` decodes to, or a datasheet
value recorded in one readable override file — and when the shield cannot deliver what a part
needs, the operator is told before the attempt, not after the failure.

**The mechanism was the real first deliverable, not any single value.** D-1 forbade part-specific
code in the generator, so Phase 197's job was to build the route by which a datasheet can beat a
decode without hardcoding anything, and then drive exactly one correction through it end to end.
`tools/datasheet_overrides.json` is that file: entries hold **only the changed fields**, each names
the datasheet it comes from and the decoded value it replaces, and the loader fails closed on an
unknown part, an unknown field, or an override that has silently become a no-op.

**All three of `build_db.py`'s part-specific hardcodes are gone.** `NMOS_TRUE_VPP_MV` moved out as
six one-row `UNSOURCED` entries with a byte-identical regeneration proving the move changed nothing.
`_AT28C_DIP24_NAMES` and `_ETYPE_RELABEL` were deleted as **defects rather than migrated as
corrections** — the distinction matters, because migrating them would have preserved a wrong answer
in a tidier place. OVR-06's census then found and named **one honest survivor** rather than
reporting the padded-empty answer the requirement's own text predicted.

**The whole-milestone database delta, measured directly against the fork point `70c92ce`:**

| | |
|---|---|
| rows in the database | 746, before and after |
| rows added or removed | **0** |
| rows changed | **25** |
| `support_status` changes | **0** |
| field changes | `electrical.vdd_mv` 15 · `unsupported_reason` 9 · `electrical.vpp_mv` 3 · `programming.pulse_duration_us` 3 · `electrical.vcc_mv` 1 · `electrical.type` 1 |

Nine of the 25 are the reworded `unsupported_reason` that fell out of the `_AT28C_DIP24_NAMES`
deletion — the message stopped naming specific parts and started describing the class. The other 16
carry every electrical and timing correction the milestone made. **No part changed support status in
either direction**, which is the claim that matters: this milestone corrected values, it did not
quietly widen or narrow what the tool offers to program.

**The decode itself was wrong, not just its outputs.** The `VPP_MV` table's `0xF0` mask was
incomplete, so 25 V and 21 V both collapsed onto 18 V. `VCC_VOLTAGES` was completed from upstream's
`xg_vcc_voltages[]` (nine more indices), and the twelve rows whose completed decode is not credible
for their part class were signed with explicit `UNSOURCED` entries, proved load-bearing by a planted
mutation. `DECODE-NOTES.md` § 9 records the general finding underneath all of it: **the voltage
word's two nibbles and the VPP byte select a programmer rail index, not a chip requirement.** That
is why the 28-row `vcc_mv == 5500` group had been unprovable since v1.32 Phase 148, and it is now
disposed of row by row.

**The rails were measured, not assumed** (Phase 199, on the operator's bench):

| path | register | deliverable at socket pin 1 |
|---|---|---|
| drop-resistor (standard VPP) | `0x188` | **17380 mV** |
| direct VPE (VPE-as-VPP) | `0x088` | **22140 mV** |

The drop path reaches **none** of the 30 rows asking ≥18 V. The direct VPE path covers **all ten**
algorithm `0x07` rows, the worst by 1140 mV of margin. So the shortfall for those ten was **removed
in firmware by routing**, not reported by a warning: `eprom_hv_route_mask` gained one comparison
against `RURP_VPP_DROP_PATH_MAX_DELIVERABLE_MV`, decided purely on path capability and **reading no
voltage at all** (D-22), with a 15-case native suite proving the decision is voltage-blind. That is
a strictly better outcome than D-4's warn-and-attempt for those rows — and it is not the same claim
RAIL-03 makes, which is why RAIL-03 is recorded as unmet rather than satisfied by proxy (§ 3).

**999.44's firmware half landed.** A `region-end` key now travels from the host's `_setup_operation`
dict, over the wire, through the parser, into `mem_util_blank_check_region`, so a write into a blank
region of a non-blank, non-erasable part succeeds while a write into a **non-blank** region is still
refused. The wire field carries an **absolute end address, not a length** — `eprom_internal_write_init_body`
is re-entered once per 8192-byte chunk and `handle->address` is the scan cursor from entry 2 onward,
so `end = handle->address + length` would have been wrong on every call but the first, a silent
correctness bug visible only on parts larger than 8 KiB. The regression case was watched RED at plan
201-02 with its transcript committed, then GREEN at 201-03's firmware commit, then confirmed on real
silicon.

## 3. The five Pending requirements, one by one

19 of 24 Complete. All five gaps are decisions with the reasoning on the record, and none is an
omission — but the milestone is `override_closeout` because a decision to not do something is still
not doing it.

**PULSE-04 (gh#70), VOLT-04 (gh#66), RAIL-05 (gh#71) — three answers written, approved and held.**
Each draft exists as a committed five-section document (`197-GH70-ANSWER.md`, `198-GH66-ANSWER.md`,
`199-GH71-ANSWER.md`), each was reviewed by the operator, and each is scanned clean of every
internal-provenance pattern — no plan ids, phase numbers, requirement or decision ids, no
`.planning/` paths — because they are public comments read by people with no repository.

The hold rests on a checkable fact rather than a preference: **nothing is pushed.**
`git branch -r --contains HEAD` resolves nothing in any of the three repositories. `post-with-beta`
would have named a version that does not exist; `post-with-commit` would have named a SHA no reader
could look up. Holding is the only route on which every claim is true at the moment it posts.

One amendment is worth recording because it was a correctness fix, not a style edit: the gh#70 draft
originally said the datasheet PDF *"is committed to this project's repository … so anyone can check
it directly."* That advertised redistributing a vendor PDF **and was false until push**. It was
removed and replaced with a `tPW` / page 4-68 citation of the PDF the reporter attached themselves.

**Whoever ships v1.40 must read `197-GH70-ANSWER.md` § "Held-pending deferral".** It is the single
consolidated list: all three issue numbers, all three draft paths, all three requirement ids, and
the four steps — fill in the real version, re-run the two text checks, post **only** the
`## Comment Body` section, record the URL and close the requirement.

**RAIL-03 — honestly unmet.** The only operator-facing shortfall signal is the pre-existing,
untouched `MSG_WARN_VPP_LOW` in `eprom_check_vpp`. It has the right shape — it names both voltages
and never refuses — and it is measurably insufficient on two independent grounds. Its 5 % trigger
window is narrower than the bench-measured +7.59 % / +7.95 % ADC-vs-meter discrepancy, so for **9 of
the 10 rescued rows** (required 18000 mV) the check stays silent while the socket is genuinely short:
a 17100 mV threshold against an 18700 mV ADC reading, both figures from the bench record. And it names
the live ADC reading, not the deliverable-maximum figure RAIL-03 asks for. The gap is named in
`DECODE-NOTES.md` § 10 and the reasoning is in `199-05-SUMMARY.md`. RAIL-04's routing fix removes the
shortfall entirely for those ten rows, which is better — and is a different claim.

**OVR-03 — enforced by the test suite, not by the generator.** Every one of the shipped override
entries carries `datasheet`, `note` and `{was, is}` per field, so the file satisfies OVR-03's literal
text. But `_validate_datasheet_overrides_shape` does not check that a cited path is git-tracked and
does not reject a `..`-escaping path; that stronger contract lives only in
`tests/test_datasheet_overrides.py`. A standalone `python tools/build_db.py` — the documented regen
command — would accept a citation the test suite rejects. Its substantive route is backlog **999.72**.
Separately and independently, the verifier found OVR-03 was **orphaned across plans**: no plan's
`requirements-completed` ever claimed it, and the two plans that declare it both defer it. Leaving it
Pending is correct on the merits either way.

  they exposed is why 999.38's calibration item stays filed.
- **215 of 297 algorithm 7/8 rows still carry an unproven 100 µs pulse.** Three were corrected. The
  rest are inventoried, not fixed, and the cost model for fixing them is stated: one entry, one
  datasheet, one row.
- **Phase 201's silicon proof is one part on one controller** — an ST M27C512 on a Leonardo with a
  Rev 2.0-class shield — and it proves criterion 1 only: a write into a *blank* region of a non-blank
  part succeeds. The refusal arm is proven in native tests, not on that bench run.

  `ROADMAP.md` is a ~8,300-line hand-authored accretion and the CLI's reorganize step would overwrite
