<objective>
Convert an additive fail-open into an additive fail-closed: make it mechanically impossible for a new
`dev test` helper to go unscanned by `tools/check_devtest_orchestrator.py`.

Purpose: `_HANDLER_FUNCTION_NAMES` fails closed only when **none** of its names match. A partial
match — `dev_test` present, a new `_sdp_leg_*` helper absent — scans successfully and silently omits
the new function, printing `PASS:` while meaning nothing about the new code. Phases 133 and 134 add
exactly that kind of helper. The comment above the list already states the obligation in prose; this
plan makes it mechanical.

Output: a test that AST-derives every module-level `_`-prefixed function referenced from `dev_test`'s
body and asserts that set is a **subset of** `_HANDLER_FUNCTION_NAMES`, plus its non-vacuity proof.

Implements GATE-10 and decision D-15, as refined by correction F-04.
</objective>

## Corrections that amend locked decisions

The phase's six corrections are tabled in full in `131-01-PLAN.md`. One governs **this** plan.

**F-04 — refines D-15.** The AST derivation must walk `dev_test`'s **body statements only**.

Measured live 2026-08-03 on `firestarter/cli_handlers.py`: a naive `ast.walk` over the whole
`FunctionDef` node — which includes `decorator_list` — yields **seven** names, one of which is
`_complete_eprom`, referenced as the `shell_complete=` argument of the `@click.argument("chip", …)`
decorator at `:2056`. `_complete_eprom` is **not** in `_HANDLER_FUNCTION_NAMES` (it is a shell
completion callback shared by 15 commands, not a `dev test` helper), so the naive derivation makes
the leg **RED on day one** — for a locator reason, not a substantive one.

Walking `dev_test.body` statements only yields exactly **six** names —`_chip_id_fields`,
`_is_interactive`, `_make_sampler`, `_resolve_write_scope`, `_sanitize_chip_token`, `_verdict_code` —
all six of which are listed. The subset holds today; the leg is green on arrival and will redden the
moment Phase 133 or 134 adds a helper without listing it.

Also measured, and the reason the assertion must be a **subset** and never an equality:
`_default_uv_write_confirm` and `_is_uv_eprom` are listed in `_HANDLER_FUNCTION_NAMES` but are not
referenced from `dev_test`'s body. An equality assertion would be RED on day one for the opposite
reason. **Direction matters**: this new leg proves *every referenced helper is listed*; the existing
`test_handler_function_names_all_resolve_to_real_callables` proves *every listed name is real*.
Together they are bidirectional.
