<objective>
Give `DiagnosticReport` a named three-valued string field for the SDP hold state, serialise it as a
tenth `to_dict()` key, render it as its own console row, and bump `SCHEMA_VERSION` to 1.3 — with a
committed assertion that no boolean anywhere in the artifact claims a protection state this family
cannot report.

The field is the only place the run's SDP outcome survives when the steps themselves are absent.
Output: the carriage half of LEG-12, the schema bump, the no-boolean gate, and D-11's cost recorded.
</objective>
