## Decisions Made

- Regenerated via the real, unmodified `python tools/build_db.py` invocation (a live
  HTTPS fetch to gitlab.com at the pinned commit) rather than pointing at the cached
  scratchpad XML copy -- the committed recipe must not depend on a path that will not
  exist for a future maintainer running it verbatim.
- Ran the blast-radius script twice: once pre-commit (`PRE_DB_REF=HEAD` override, the
  "stop and investigate before forcing the diff through" check the plan's action
  prescribes) and once post-commit (default invocation, `HEAD~1`, matching the plan's
  own literal `<verify>` command) -- both agree exactly, giving both an early-stop
  safety check and a standing, literally-reproducible proof.
- `infoic_page_size_raw` is deliberately keyed differently from the existing curated
  `programming.page_size` (`_PAGE_SIZE_BY_PART`) -- same English word, two different
  provenance sources (raw upstream attribute vs. datasheet-curated), documented at both
  the `build_db.py` decode site and the new `diff_db.py` rule so neither is ever
  conflated with the other.
