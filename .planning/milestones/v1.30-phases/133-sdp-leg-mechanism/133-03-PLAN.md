<objective>
Give the engine its SDP op vocabulary and a fail-closed dispatch arm for it, positioned so that the
seven already-shipped ops provably never evaluate it.

Purpose: this is the vocabulary Phase 134's four-step leg is built on — ROADMAP Phase 134's
`Depends on` line names the `_SDP_OPS` dispatch arm verbatim — and it is the phase's only chance to
add an op to this module without inheriting the fail-open shape LEG-15 exists to remove. The module's
own history is the argument for every choice here: before Phase 121 an unmapped op fell through to
`operator.erase_eprom()` and reported OK, and `_MULTI_RUN_OPS` once shipped documented-but-dead with
zero references tree-wide. So the new frozenset must be referenced by live code, the new arm must
refuse rather than fall through, and the refusal must be tested.

Nothing in this plan is user-visible. `derive_plan` is NOT taught to emit SDP steps (that is Phase 134,
LEG-01/02); in this phase the ops are reachable only from a directly-constructed `Step` in a test, and
from the cleanup registry plan 133-04 adds.

Output: `OP_SDP_LOCK`, `OP_SDP_UNLOCK`, `_SDP_OPS`, `_dispatch_sdp`, arm 5 of `_dispatch_step`, the
`_DESTRUCTIVE_OPS` membership asymmetry, and D-13b's zero-added-branching-cost sentinel.
</objective>
