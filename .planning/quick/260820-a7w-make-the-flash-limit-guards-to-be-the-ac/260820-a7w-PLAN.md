<objective>
Make both flash-limit guards report the real flash size of each microcontroller: 32768 B
on all three AVR targets (ATmega328P, ATmega328PB, ATmega32U4 all have 32 KiB).

Purpose: the ceilings in force today are bootloader-reduced (uno 32256, uno328pb 32384,
leonardo 28672), so the reported headroom understates the silicon. The user asked for the
real MCU size and reaffirmed the request after being told, in plain terms, that this
removes the linker's protection over the bootloader region -- on `leonardo` it opens the
top 4096 B occupied by Caterina, so firmware that later grows past 28672 B would be linked
into and flashed over the bootloader.

Implement exactly that. Do NOT narrow it, do NOT add a compensating "bootloader-safe"
second guard, and do NOT shrink the change to avoid the consequence. DO make the
consequence legible: a comment at each override site naming the reserve being given up per
target, and a `meta` note in the baseline recording why the totals moved and what
protection was traded away. That is documentation, not a new gate.

Output: the two guards moved in lockstep (PlatformIO's reported ceiling and the recorded
`flash_total` invariant), three committed cold-build logs as the value source, and the four
default-mode test legs severed onto a fixture family that carries the new ceiling.
</objective>
