<objective>
Find out what the RURP shield's high-voltage rails actually deliver at the socket, on the one shield
revision gh#71's reporter was using.

Everything downstream of this plan depends on one number: the multimeter reading at socket pin 1 on
the drop-resistor path, with the pot at maximum. gh#71 reports 17.8 V on VPP and 22.7 V on VPE, but
both are firmware ADC readings taken with no socket-routing bit asserted, and backlog 999.38
measured that ADC roughly 7.5 percent high. The real rails were nearer 16.6 V and 21.1 V. Nobody has
ever put a meter on socket pin 1 with the rail held and the chip out.

D-07 says the measurement wins outright. If the meter disagrees with the operator's provisional
18 V, the meter is what ships. D-08 makes this plan a hard predecessor of plan `199-03` for exactly
that reason: a host threshold written against 18000 before the reading exists would make D-07
unobservable.

**Edge-probe accounting for this plan: 2 of the phase's 8 probe-surfaced items.** RAIL-01 precision
is resolved explicit and authored as a plain `must_haves.truths` string. RAIL-01 concurrency is
resolved backstop and authored as a structured marker, because the guarantee it states — that no rail
is left energized when a window ends — is observable at the bench and not from a committed artifact.
The remaining six are carried by plans `199-03` (3), `199-04` (1) and `199-05` (2).

**This plan halts for a human three times and must not be run unattended.** Standing bench rule 7 in
`.planning/milestones/v1.34-artifacts/PROCEDURE.md` forbids running this procedure under any
auto-advance mode, and project experience records that such modes auto-approve human-verify gates
while `autonomous: false` does nothing to stop them. Every checkpoint below is a
`checkpoint:human-action` carrying `gate="blocking-human"`, which is never bypassed in any mode.

Purpose: turn RAIL-01 from a citation into a measurement, and produce the one integer every other
plan in this phase reads.
Output: `199-BENCH-RECORD.md`, carrying four named figure lines, the method, the operator statements
verbatim, and the honesty limits the method creates.
</objective>
