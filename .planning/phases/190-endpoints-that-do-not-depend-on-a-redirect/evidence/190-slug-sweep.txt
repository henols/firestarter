Scope: the firestarter_app working tree only (tracked files), from its own
root. The naive sweep `grep -rn "henols/firestarter" | grep -v
"firestarter_fw\|firestarter_app\|firestarter_prom"` under-reports: it
filters whole LINES, not matches, so a line naming both the bare slug and
one of the renamed forms in the same sentence is silently dropped
(RESEARCH Pitfall 1 -- measured 6 of 7 real sites with the naive pipeline
before this phase). The pattern below is boundary-aware instead: it
requires the bare slug be followed by a non-identifier character or the
end of the line, so it cannot match `firestarter_fw`, `firestarter_app` or
`firestarter_prom`, all of which extend the slug with `_`.

A zero bare-slug reading paired with a zero control would be a failed gate,
not a pass -- it would mean the pattern itself matched nothing, which is
indistinguishable from a clean tree without the control. Here the control
returns its expected non-zero count (8, matching 190-02's own measurement
of the same pairing), so the zero bare-slug reading is a genuine clean
result: URL-01's constants (190-01), URL-03's derivation and pin (190-02),
and D-04's two remaining prose sites (submit.py's comment and the app
repo's own .planning/codebase/INTEGRATIONS.md, both cleared in 190-02) are
all confirmed clear as of this commit.

---------------------------------------------
- The meta repository (/workspaces, outside firestarter_app). That sweep is

  .planning/codebase/ documents are explicitly out of this phase's scope
  (190-CONTEXT.md "Out of scope").

  stale copies of constants.py; `git grep` does not see it because it is
  untracked, and it is explicitly out of scope per 190-CONTEXT.md.
