## The measured layer shape

The existing `tests/golden/wire_dict_expected_deltas_149.json` has two top-level keys, `deltas` and
`meta`. Its `meta` block holds `decision`, `honesty`, `how_to_update`, `phase` and `provenance`. Its
delta keys are the manufacturer, the part number and the record's positional index inside that
manufacturer's array, joined by a pipe. Its values are a single-key mapping of the wire key to its
value. It holds 18 entries, all `0x0D` rows.

The new layer holds **25** entries: the 27 protocol `0x05` rows minus the two that already emitted a
`page-size` value before this phase. Those two are `WINBOND/W29C020,W29C020C,W29C022` at 128 and
`WINBOND/W29C040,W29C042` at 256, and they must NOT appear in the new layer -- the non-vacuity
assertion in task 2 is the leg that catches their inclusion.

**The positional index is the fragile part of every key.** Row order is stable for a pinned upstream
input, but the index must still be generated from the regenerated database rather than transcribed.
The golden's own provenance note states this rule for the existing layer.

| two layers carrying the same field | Order-independent composition holds only while the layers are key-disjoint |
