## Decisions

- **D-07 (fork F7): the keep-or-revert threshold, pre-registered here, before the bench task.** The lease is KEPT only if all three clauses hold on the reference part and board class:
  1. it removes **at least 15.0 percent** of the plan's median wall clock;
  2. **and** the removed time is **at least five times** the measurement's own spread (max minus min, taken over the wider of the two arms);
  3. **and** no step's verdict differs from the pre-lease arm for the same physical outcome.

  Below any clause, the single lease commit is reverted and the number is recorded. Rationale for 15 percent: the derived connect-term saving at roughly thirty connects and a 2.607 s median is far above it, so a measured result *below* 15 percent is itself evidence the lease is not doing what it was built to do rather than evidence that 15 percent was too demanding. Clause 2 stops a noisy measurement from deciding a structural question. Clause 3 makes DEVTEST-03 a gate on SESS-01 rather than a separate concern — a faster run that changed a verdict has not paid, it has cost. **This threshold is Claude's discretion, adopted explicitly and recorded as such (RESEARCH Assumptions Log A2): the structure — pre-registered, three clauses, one of them about fidelity rather than time — matters more than the number.** Rating: `reversible`.
- **D-08 (RESEARCH Open Question 3): `.planning/ROADMAP.md`'s phase-table cell marking Phase 206 as not bench-gated is stale and is corrected in this phase.** Reason: it contradicts the same file's own success criterion 5 for the same phase, and SESS-02's requirement text. The cell was written before the requirement wording was finalised. Resolved in favour of the requirement, and SESS-02 is not satisfied by derivation. Rating: `reversible`.
- **D-10 (RESEARCH Open Question 4): the seated part is operator-owned and is asked for, never assumed.** Reason: whether the socket is occupied and by what cannot be determined without energizing the board, and the operator owns chip handling and shield-revision identification. W27C512 is the recommended reference part for continuity with `205-BENCH-MATRIX.md` and `205-SESSION-COST.md`; AT28C256 is the second choice because its SDP leg makes it the overhead-dominated outlier where a lease pays most. The operator decides. Rating: `reversible`.
