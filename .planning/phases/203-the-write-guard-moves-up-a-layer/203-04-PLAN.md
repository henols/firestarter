every added paragraph costs snapshot lines in two places.

Write `203-SESSION-COST.md`: the added wall-clock this phase costs, derived from recorded
measurements, with every term's provenance on the page.

The phase is bench-no, and the in-repo connect-cost harness needs a real board — it resolves a port
and returns early without one — so no new hardware measurement is available and none may be invented.
The honest route is a derivation over figures that were measured, each cited to the artifact that
measured it.

**The document must state, in this order:**

1. **What is being counted.** Every operation opens and closes its own port; the operation context
   disconnects in its `finally`. A guarded `write` is therefore two port opens where it used to be
   one, and a guarded `write --verify` is three. Each open resets an Uno-class board.

2. **The connect term, cited.** The per-open medians from the Phase 176 measurement, with the board
   class, the sample count, the min and max, the structural floor, and the date and app revision they
   were taken against. Do not round them silently; quote them as recorded.

3. **The derived totals.** Two extra opens for a guarded `write --verify`, one extra for a guarded
   plain `write`, stated per board class. Mark the arithmetic as this document's own reasoning —
   the medians are measured, the addition is derived.

4. **The abort term, cited.** The bounded per-abort cost from the Phase 202 read-abort answer, which
   applies on a refusal, where the guard stops the read in flight.

5. **The read term, cited and hedged.** The only in-repo throughput figures come from a single
   operator log, and the note carrying them says so in its own words. Quote that caveat alongside the
   figures. A rate presented without it becomes a benchmark the next reader will trust.

6. **The explicit non-measurement statement.** Say in plain words that Phase 203 took no new hardware
   measurement, that the phase was scoped bench-no, and that the figures are a derivation over prior

   the reason D-17 asked for it.

   before-figure, and its error bar is the single-sample read term, not the connect term.

8. **The alternative that remains open.** A bench run would replace the derivation with a
   measurement. It was not taken because the phase is bench-no; the operator can commission one at any
   time and this document should be superseded rather than amended if that happens.

Add a provenance table at the end, one row per term: term, value, measured or derived, and the
