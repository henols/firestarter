<objective>
Pin the guarded set to exactly the five protocol families the firmware blank-checks at write-init
today, with the named exemptions carrying their reasoning at the exemption site, and with a test that
fails if the set narrows or widens. Prove the bypass flag and the erase-exemption re-arming behave as
decided, prove `dev test` and `dev write-cycle` are behaviourally unchanged, and close the folded
negative-start-address todo on its host half.

Purpose: the predicate is the whole safety net after Phase 205, and this pinning test is the only
thing that will detect a later drift. Amended WRITE-02 makes it a narrows-or-widens obligation, which
is strictly stronger than the requirement it replaced.

Output: `tests/test_write_blank_guard_pinning.py`, the named-exemption documentation in
`write_blank_guard.py`, `require_non_negative_address` and `NegativeStartAddressError`.
</objective>
